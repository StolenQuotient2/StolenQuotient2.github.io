Years ago, when the world was anew, the God-King cast a special spell, called the Veil. The Veil has some special properties; it's incredibly hard to detect, and even harder to target with a spell of your own. Its effects permeate the entire plane that Panops exists on.   
The Veil does a few key things, mostly things that keep the natural order of the world and the planet. For example, it maintains typical weather cycles, but it also prevents the planet from being knocked out of its orbit with the sun. Most critically, in addition to the base restrictions of mana and spellcasting, the Veil introduces a number of other restrictions on the types of spell effects that can be created:

- You cannot travel backwards in time more than a few seconds  
- You cannot alter events of the past that occurred more than a few seconds ago, nor create past events that would have occurred more than a few seconds ago  
- You cannot look into the past to see events you were not present for  
- You cannot travel to dimensions or alternate timelines outside this one  
- You cannot see or travel to the World beyond Death  
- You cannot leave the World beyond Death on your own  
- You cannot remember what the World beyond Death looks like, or what resides in it.

The Veil is considered just a natural part of the laws of magic, and the fact that it is, in fact, a spell of its own is the most heavily-guarded secret of the Church. Without overwhelmingly special circumstances, player characters will have no knowledge that the Veil exists. These rules are treated in Panops as just natural restrictions that spells have. 

### Veilbreaks

NPCs of exceptional power—or if the Veil is damaged or destroyed in some way, players—will be able to cast spells which are one or more kinds of *Veilbreak*. Each violates a specific set of rules of the Veil. If an NPC casts a Veilbreak, they should only be able to use that kind of Veilbreak once. 

The types of Veilbreak are as listed, in descending order of possibility:
- *Chronobreak* – Violate the laws of time. Travel back in time an exceptional amount, perfectly divine historical events, or rewrite history.
- *Deathbreak* — Break the gates of the World Beyond Death. See the true nature of the WBD, remember a past self, or bring yourself back from death. 
- *Planebreak* — Explore the multiverse. Travel to other dimensions, other timelines, or the World Beyond Death. 
- *Worldbreak* — Change the planet. Move continents, change climates, or alter the planet's orbit. {Create a moon.}
- *Realitybreak* — Alter reality. Change the laws of physics, metaphysics, or spellcasting.
