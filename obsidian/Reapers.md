The Reapers are nominally a charity group for troubled youth, providing food and shelter to those in need. However, they also operate as an almost militia-like entity with their ability to summon mages to complete tasks. The combination of these two things makes the Reapers known for arming troublemakers, and puts them commonly at odds with the Dreamers, the Council, and the Church.  
The Reapers are formed almost entirely from displaced children from the Drain, and due to the lack of government support for teenagers in general, the Reapers often turn to theft, bounty hunting, and seeking out powerful magical items in order to pay bills and keep the group operating. The Reapers provide housing, food, and shelter to all of their members using a combination of legally and illegally obtained funds. 
The Reapers have little hierarchy. Particularly powerful or influential Reapers will take on the role of caretaker for the Reaper base of a specific location. The Reapers are densely packed within cities where the bases exist, but the bases themselves are not as widespread as Council or Dreamer institutions.
