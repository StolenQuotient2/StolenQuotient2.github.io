The Reapers are nominally a charity group for troubled youth, providing food and shelter to those in need. However, they also teach to anyone interested the art of Weaving, and so they also operate as an almost militia-like entity with their ability to summon mages to complete tasks. The combination of these two things makes the Reapers known for arming troublemakers, and puts them commonly at odds with the Dreamers, the Council, and the Church.   
The Reapers have little hierarchy. Particularly powerful or influential Reapers will take on the role of caretaker for the Reaper base of a specific location. The Reapers are densely packed within cities where the bases exist, but the bases themselves are not as widespread as Council or Dreamer institutions.
