*The Song of Creation is a collection of six verses, musical ideas that echo the very structure of magic and shake the world to its core. Knowing just a single verse makes you fiercely strong, but with critical flaws. Knowing parts of the entire song gives you the power of gods, and singing the full song gives you power unimaginable.*   

---
These six tomes cannot be destroyed, only changed hands. Possessing one makes you a target of a vast hoard of treasure seekers, as well as the Church.   
While in possession of a tome, you may bind to it to gain its power, but you will lose its effects the moment the tome leaves your possession. You may also Study a tome; if you have the tome for at least a month, and are using it and reading from it actively, you no longer need the tome to use its power.   
You must declare at the start of your action if you wish to use one or more Verses to cast a spell. If you do, you start to contract the consequences of any verses you use. {The exact mechanical nature of the consequences of each Verse is up to the GM, but should be in the spirit of the flavorwise consequence.}

---
###  ***The Verse of Chaos***
*This Verse is about the unburdened, the wild, and the free. This Verse is about chance, about risk and reward. This Verse is about all that is unbound by thought.*    

If you learn this verse, you may treat Luck as a core aspect if it is not already. When you make a Whim roll, restore 1 mana for each die that landed on Luck. Unless you also know the Verse of Motive, using this Verse to cast spells will cause you to start to become more and more uncertain about your actions, your beliefs, and your goals. You will start to feel like you are no longer in control of your body and your mind.

---
### 	***The Verse of Motive***
*This Verse is about the furious, the brave and the hopeful. This Verse is about belief, about fidelity and sacrilege. This Verse is about all that keeps the world turning.*  

If you learn this verse, you may treat Confidence as a core aspect if it is not already. When you make a Whim roll, restore 1 mana for each die that landed on Confidence. Unless you also know the Verse of Schemes, using this Verse to cast spells will cause you to become so single-mindedly focused on a goal that you will struggle to do anything that doesn’t directly progress towards that goal. You will have trouble considering long-term consequences and planning, opting to instead act first and think later. 

---
### 	***The Verse of Schemes***

*This Verse is about the smart, the patient and the creative. This Verse is about thought, complex and intrusive. This Verse is about all that is studied and learned.*  

If you learn this verse, you may treat Preparation as a core aspect if it is not already. When you make a Whim roll, restore 1 mana for each die that landed on Preparation. Unless you also know the Verse of Avarice, using this Verse to cast spells will cause you to overthink severely. You will question yourself, others, the nature of the very universe.  You will get so lost in thought that you will be unable to move from your chair. 

---
### 	***The Verse of Avarice***

*This Verse is about the bold, the tough, and the flashy. This Verse is about power, stalwart and destructive. This Verse is about all that can be achieved.*    

If you learn this verse, you may treat Strength as a core aspect if it is not already. When you make a Whim roll, restore 1 mana for each die that landed on Strength. Unless you also know the Verse of Control, using this Verse to cast spells will cause you to overindulge and forget your limits. You will put your full weight behind every punch, put your life on the line, and cause catastrophe for the undeserving. You will lose your wit, and become short-tempered.

---
### 	***The Verse of Control*** 

*This Verse is about the focused, the mindful and the dominant. This Verse is about authority, about virtuosity and demand. This Verse is about all that can be manipulated for gain.*   

If you learn this verse, you may treat Will as a core aspect if it is not already. When you make a Whim roll, restore 1 mana for each die that landed on Will. Unless you also know the Verse of Chaos, using this Verse to cast spells will cause immense paranoia. You will demand to be in charge of every aspect of your environment: the people, the places, the magic. You will demand respect but will not return it. 

---
### 	***The Verse of Balance***

*This Verse is about the even, the equal, and the unified. This Verse is about morality, about good and evil. This Verse is about all things as one thing.*   

If you learn this verse, you may treat Arcana as a core aspect. If you know any other Verses, using this Verse to cast spells will stay much of the effects of the others’ continued use. If you know no other Verses, using this Verse to cast spells will leave you with a feeling of deep emptiness. You may find it peaceful, you may find it boring. You will eventually be completely even-tempered, and will be unphased by any and all situations.
