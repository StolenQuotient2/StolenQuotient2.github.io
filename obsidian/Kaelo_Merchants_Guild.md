A guild of merchants who sell to adventurers on the Kaelo archipelago. The core idea of the guild is to regulate what items are sold to adventurers and for what price. The Guild also protects merchants from rogue adventurers who steal or destroy to get items. Since there is no immediate Council presence on the Kaelo archipelago, the Guild serves as the keeper of order on the islands.
