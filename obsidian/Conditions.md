## Conditions

Rather than a health point total, each character can only suffer a certain number of *Conditions* before they are taken out of play. Conditions represent both injuries as well as other hinderances on the player characters. They range from minor problems, such as being distracted, to severe and life-threatening problems, like stab wounds. 

Each condition comes with a *Level* between 1 and 4. When a player takes 6 total levels of conditions, they are removed from play, and suffer [Shock, Trauma, or Death]. 

### Applying Conditions

Conditions arise in play as a natural consequence of the narrative--when you fail actions, trigger traps, or otherwise get into bad situations, your GM may apply a condition to you. If you're tangled in vines, maybe its a Restrained (Level 2). If a firework went off in your face, maybe its Singed (Level 1) and Blinded (Level 2). If you're still trying to flirt with that cute girl from the [Talents]({% link obsidian/Talents.md %}) section, then maybe its Flustered (Level 1000).
