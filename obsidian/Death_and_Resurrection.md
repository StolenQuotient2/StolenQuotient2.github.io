Dying is generally something people want to avoid. Unfortunately, it tends to happen whether you like it or not, especially if you’re someone who regularly puts themselves in dangerous situations. When you meet your untimely end, you will travel to the World beyond Death. There are differing opinions about the nature of this world–whether it is a paradise or a pit of suffering or nothingness incarnate–but one thing that mages have discovered is that you can bring people back.   
Resurrection is a special kind of action. First you need to cast a spell, which costs 16 mana. Then, to actually perform a resurrection, calculate Fate for the upcoming roll:  
Increase your Fate by 1 for each of the following conditions you meet:

- The target died recently (less than an hour ago)  
- The target died *very* recently (less than a minute ago)  
- You witnessed the death of your target  
- You have a personal connection to the target   
- 

Decrease your Fate by 1 for each of the following conditions you meet:

- The target has been dead for a long time (more than a few hours)  
- The target has been dead for a *really* long time (more than a few days)  
- The target has been dead for a ***really*** long time (more than a few months)

Then make a Whim roll and apply fate. If you score between 2 and 4, nothing happens. If you score 1 or less, the soul of the target is lost to the World Beyond Death and they cannot be resurrected anymore. If you score 5 or more, the target is resurrected.   
	When you resurrect a target, you may spend Focus to do the following effects:

- The target revives with all injuries healed (rather than the injuries that they had before they went unconcious)  
- The target revives with all mana restored  
- The target revives with a third of their maximum Focus (rounded up) restored
