*Encounters* are the active-play side of Misfits, where you'll explore the world, take actions, suffer consequences, and aim for your goals. This is in comparison to [Recess]({% link obsidian/Recess.md %}), which is the passive-play side of Misfits, where you spend resources in order to gain information and items without playing through the scenes directly.

Encounters have two possible states: An Encounter can be in (and starts with) *Free Play*, where players can explore the world and take actions freely without significant risk of consequence, or a granular turn-based action system. Alternatively, an Encounter can be in a *[Situation]({% link obsidian/Situations.md %})*, where there are potential consequences and a reasonable chance of failure, and there is a more granular turn system to support more complicated environments. A single encounter may have multiple instances of both Free Play and Situations. 

Mechanically, the differences between Free Play and Situations aren't enormous. In fact, Free Play follows the exact same conventions as Situations with the following omissions:
- There are no Action Point costs
- There are no Threats
- Actions can be Prepared and then immediately executed. 

Moreover, actions taken in Free Play will very often not have associated [Action Rolls]({% link obsidian/Rolling_Dice.md %}), whereas actions taken in a Situation will very often have a roll associate with them.

Essentially, Free Play still follows the fundamental premise of "the players will take a (standard) action, and then the scene will develop".
