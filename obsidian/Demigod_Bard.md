* ### ***Displacer ([Demigod]({% link obsidian/Demigod.md %}) \+ [Bard]({% link obsidian/Bard.md %})): \[Kotori\]***

  * *“Quote”*  
  * *The Soul is a fickle thing, and the Technique of the Displacer takes full advantage. Resonant frequencies can temporarily displace a soul from its body, and taking advantage of this is a dangerous but powerful strategy.*   
  * *The Technique of the Displacer is forbidden by the Dreamers, the Council and the Church*  
* The following are abilities you may take as a Displacer:  
* {E} GIMMICK: At any time, you may spend 1 Focus to remove any physical restrictions on your movement or spellcasting. This is extremely unsettling to anyone who views it. {its kinda like dislocating your thumb to get out of handcuffs? But like. With your soul.}  
  * {E} Through Silenced Skeins: When you successfully cast a spell in your domain that affects a single target, you may prevent that target from casting spells, and you may pay 3 mana to dispel any sustained spell effects they have. You may maintain this ability as a sustained spell effect, but only one instance at a time.    
  * {?} In Hushed Whispers: Your soul is imperceptible. To those who can see magical auras, it is not clear whether or not you are a spellcaster, or even alive at all. Spellcasters cannot target you with spells if they cannot see you, including divining your location. Should you die, there is a \-2 Fate decrement to anyone trying to resurrect you, as your soul is hard to find  in the World Beyond Death.   {“This is a hard nerf if I’m intending to die more than once” –one of my alpha testers}  
  * {S} With Softened Steps: Once between Recesses, when you assist an ally, you may choose to have yourself and that ally be untargetable by spells for a round. This effect nullifies any Threats that involve spells targeting you or that ally directly. Addressing Threats that affect areas you or your ally are in have \+1 Fate.
