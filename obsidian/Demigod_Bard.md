*The Soul is a fickle thing, and the Technique of the Displacer takes full advantage. Resonant frequencies can temporarily displace a soul from its body, and taking advantage of this is a dangerous but powerful strategy.*  

---
### ***Displacer ([Demigod]({% link obsidian/Demigod.md %}) \+ [Bard]({% link obsidian/Bard.md %})): \[Kotori\]***


- **DISPLACER1 (Starting Ability):** When you take DISPLACER1, lose the Implicit Divinity (Demigod) and Harmony (Bard) abilities. If you had the Implicit Divinity (Demigod) ability, retain your previous domain. Additionally, gain the With Uncanny Dislocation (Displacer) ability. When you cast a spell while manipulating your own or another person's soul, you may spend 1 Focus to gain 1 Fate. 

- **With Uncanny Dislocation:** You may spend 1 Focus to manipulate your soul to remove any physical restrictions on your movement or spellcasting. This is extremely upsetting to anyone who sees it. 

- **Through Silenced Skeins:** When you successfully cast a spell with a single human target, you may manipulate their soul to prevent them from casting spells. Treat this effect as a Sustained spell (in your domain, if you have one) while it is active, and you may only have one such instance of this effect at a time. 

- **In Hushed Whispers:** Your soul is imperceptible. To those who can see magical auras, it is not clear whether or not you are a spellcaster, or even alive at all. Spellcasters cannot target you with spells if they cannot see you, including divining your location. Should you die, there is a \-2 Fate decrement to anyone trying to resurrect you, as your soul is especially hard to find in the World Beyond Death. {“This is a hard nerf if I’m intending to die more than once” –Sable, one of my alpha testers, and the initial designer of this Technique.}

- **With Subtle Steps:** Once between Recesses, when you participate in an Entwined action, you may manipulate your and the other participants' souls so that you all are untargetable by spells for the current and next action. When an enemy would try to target you with a spell, they cannot; however, you may spend 1 Focus to manipulate their soul and change the targets as you choose.
