Formally, the Exalted do not exist. A secretive organization run by \[Teia Kalesko\], the Exalted are similar to the Reapers in many ways, in that their targets are the corrupt authorities of the world, but they are much smaller in number, and operate on a much larger scale. The Exalted’s primary function is to keep \[Aries\] and the Church in check.   
Each member of the Exalted is personally selected by Teia, and is honed into some of the worlds most effective mages, combatants, and intelligence agents. Due to similarities between the two groups, Teia often recruits from the Reapers. The Exalted are taught to be perfectly covert, and the only way to identify one of them is through a specially enchanted \[Exalted’s Pin\]. Nearly all who know the \[Duskwalker\] technique are part of the Exalted, and Teia goes to extreme lengths to make sure the Technique is not discovered by the Council or the Church.
