For better or worse, you will be known by some number of other people. When those people are part of a major faction or organization, this is represented by a Reputation score. Reputation has six levels, ranging from \+3 to \-2, and each level tells you how members of that faction/the faction as a whole will respond to your presence. The level also represents a bonus you get to any Connect rolls you make with members of the associated faction.   
The table below shows what support or attacks you can expect to receive from a faction given your reputation with them. 

| Respected (+3)      | You can easily draw up significant support in the form of people, time, or resources from this faction. Many faction members will take your word at face-value, and see you as an authority on a number of topics. You are expected to uphold the ideals of the faction you have a significant reputation in. |

| Neutral/Unknown (0) |                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| Hostile (-1)        |                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| Nemeses (-2)        | You will be treated as hostile and dangerous. People of this faction will keep their distance, both literally and metaphorically. You are to be kept as far from important facilities and resources as possible.                                                                                              |

## **Notable Faction Interactions**

- The *Reapers* tend to make their home bases from abandoned *Dreamer* schools, as they already have much of what they need from a location. The *Dreamers* believe that the *Reapers* are going out of their way to shut down schools in order to turn them into their buildings. The *Reapers* deny these allegations. The *Nightmares* quietly avoid comment.   
- The *Reapers* and the *Council* are very often at odds. *Reapers* are often accused of crimes without basis and arrested. The *Wardens* spend much of their time cataloging so-called “crime scenes” and bringing them as evidence for the eventual release of any captured *Reapers*. This has marginally decreased the number of false arrests that the *Council* makes, but also has increased the number of actual crimes the *Reapers* commit, with the assumption that the *Wardens* will break them out.   
- There are routine raids by the *Council* against the *Silver Kings*, as they attempt to take back the cities that the *Kings* have claimed for themselves. The *Kings* continue to keep the *Council* at bay, and continue to sell weapons to others who wish to fight the *Council* themselves. This continues to grow more and more violent, and there are rumors that *Enforcement* will eventually get involved. A large-scale conflict between *Enforcement* and the *Kings* would likely result in the destruction of one or more of the occupied cities.
