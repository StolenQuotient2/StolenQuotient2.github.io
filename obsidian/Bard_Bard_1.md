## **Idol ([Bard]({% link obsidian/Bard.md %}) \+ [Bard]({% link obsidian/Bard.md %}))** 


- **Phenomenon (Starting Ability)**: When you cast a spell, you may begin a *Performance*. The performance lasts until you start a new one, are incapacitated, or run out of mana. You may take a 1AP Swift action to grant the effect of your current Performance to an ally until the Performance ends by paying the associated mana cost. You start with 3 Performances of your choice, with their details listed under the Songbook (Idol) ability.  

* **Songbook**: You may take this ability any number of times. Each time you take this ability, learn a new Performance, and/or exchange up to two of your old Performances for new ones. The list of all performances is below, with their associated mana cost in parentheses. 
	* *Mage Rage*: Injuries you inflict are Severe.
	* *Savor the Moment*: Actions you take cost +1 AP, but gain +1 Fate.

* **Come Out Singing**: When you start a Performance, you may give an ally the effect of the performance for the rest of the round. You do not have to pay the cost for this instance of the Performance’s effect. 

* **Resonate**: Whenever you take a Whim roll and your dice roll a *Full House* (Three of one number, Two of another), score 5 for that Whim Roll. Then, you may give the effect of your current Performance to an ally without paying the associated mana cost. If you don’t, or can’t, instead restore 2 mana to an ally.
