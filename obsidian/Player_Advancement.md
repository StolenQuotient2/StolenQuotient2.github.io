As a reward for completing significant objectives, your GM will grant you EXP, typically in increments of 1. This EXP can be spent during Recess in order to advance your character in a variety of ways. 

When you take the [Advance] measure in Recess, you may spend your EXP in the following ways. 
- 1 EXP: Gain a [Talent]({% link obsidian/Talents.md %}) or a special ability from your [Origin]({% link obsidian/Origins.md %}) (or from a Technique, if you have one). 
- 2 EXP: Increase your maximum mana by 2, or your maximum Focus by 1. This increase comes with the associated mana and focus (so the maximum mana/focus is "full" and not "empty").
- 4 EXP: Gain the starting ability of a [Technique of the Masters]({% link obsidian/Techniques_Title_page.md %}).
