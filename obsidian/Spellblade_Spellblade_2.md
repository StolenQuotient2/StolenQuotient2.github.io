* ### ***Runedancer ([Spellblade]({% link obsidian/Spellblade.md %}) \+ Spellblade): \[\]***

  * *The Technique of the Runedancer*   
  * *The Technique of the*   
* The following are abilities you may take as a Runedancer:  
* GIMMICK: Making an attack with your spellblade prepares a spell in your domain. Casting a spell in your domain prepares an attack made with a spellblade. Only one of these can happen each round.  
* Whenever you cast a spell, note its spell family. If at any point you cast a spell whose spell family is already on this list, reset the list. When this list includes all four spell families (Wreck, Aid, Hinder, Mystify), reset the list, and gain the following bonuses: your next action costs 0AP, treats Arcana as a core aspect, and any spells you cast during this action have their mana cost halved.   
  * RUNEDANCER1: Whenever you make an attack with your spellblade, you may reset the list in your GIMMICK. When you do, any Whim rolls you make for the subsequent action have a minimum score equal to the number of items you erased from the list.   
  * RUNEDANCER2: While your list contains only Wreck and/or Hinder, attacks you make with your spellblade pierce through nonmagical defenses, and are more effective against magical ones. While your list contains only Aid and/or Mystify, spells you cast in your domain that target allies cost \-1AP to cast.   
  * Flurry: You do not have to pay Focus for taking 3 or more actions in a round. While you have both cast a spell in your domain and made an attack with your spellblade, you may score *Straights* during your Whim rolls, where the length of the straight corresponds to your final score. If you do this during an Action Roll, ignore any Fate bonuses if you choose to score this way.
