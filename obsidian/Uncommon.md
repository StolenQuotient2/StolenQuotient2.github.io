**Glint Canisters** (Weapons)  
Nicknamed "birthday cans" by some council and enforcement members, this invention comes from Mena herself. These grenades require lengthy preparation to create, but when done properly, can deliver spells as an aerosolized gas. They're most often used to deliver hinder spells on a large scale for crowd control or breach purposes, and can be identified by a distinctive heat shimmer effect.

**Veilsmoke** (Weapon)  
A small metallic orb. When thrown on the ground, it releases a pitch-dark smoke in all directions. The smoke completely blocks sight. Additionally, the smoke blocks certain magical actions: anyone inside the smoke cannot be divined or seen using magical effects, magic items which require magic to activate are disabled, and anyone inside of the smoke cannot Attune.

**Shared Suffering** (Weapon)  
Whenever an enemy takes an injury, a second enemy nearby also takes the same injury. Whenever you take an injury, a nearby ally also takes that injury. If there is no ally nearby, you take that injury a second time.

**Blessed Thermo**s (Support)  
Practical and stylish. Any liquid you store in it is purified, removing any poisons or diseases. Once between Recesses, you can take a sip and gain +1 max mana. This effect does not apply multiple times if you have multiple of the item.

**Firelight Amulet** (Oddity)  
A firelight amulet is a large necklace with a multicolored gem hanging from it. The gem is red on one side, and yellow on the other. Holding the red side up to a source of fire projects light from the other side, and holding the yellow side up to a source of light projects fire from the red side. The amulet also amplifies the intensity by a moderate amount, and chaining multiple amulets together can turn a small flame into a raging wildfire, or the light from a lantern into the light from a star.

**Fascinator** (Support)  
A small clip or hat, usually worn in the hair but may be adapted for use as a pin or brooch; made of something colorful or shiny, usually. Catches the attention of a target to lightly mesmerize them, for purposes of suggestion or distraction.   

---
**Cat’s Reflexes** (Guard)  
Plain shoes that grant the user improved balance and grip well even on extremely steep or slick surfaces. The user is also more likely to land on their feet when dropping or falling from any height.   

---
**Pathstone** (Support)  
A pathstone is a large, circular disc covered in runes. It is usually set in the ground of whatever location it is placed in. After stepping on the pathstone for the first time, spells you cast to teleport yourself or objects to the Pathstone always succeed, as long as the total volume of objects teleporting fits within the footprint of the pathstone.
