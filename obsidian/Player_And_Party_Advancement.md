#### **Level** 

There are three types of advancement for characters in *Misfits*. The first is your level, which is not character-specific, but applies to the entire party. Leveling works on a milestone system, and you should level up when you make progress towards the overarching goal on your Adventure Sheet. There are extra details on each of those sheets to describe potentially important milestones in your adventure.  When the party levels up, each character gains 2 maximum mana and 1 maximum Focus. If you choose to start a campaign at a higher level, give the characters these advancements. 

#### **Experience**

Another form of advancement is the Experience (EXP) track, which is 5 long.  When a player’s track is full, they reset it to 0 and gain their choice of an origin ability or talent. At the start of a campaign, give the players one of these advancements per level they start at above the first (so at level 2, they get 1, etc.).  
Players gain EXP individually, at the end of each session. Each player has four EXP triggers, determined by their Origin, Drive, and Aspects. At the end of each session, answer each of the four questions associated with your character, and gain 1EXP for each question you answer “Yes” to. If for some reason you would gain 0EXP this way, gain 1EXP instead. See below for the specific triggers for each specific Origin, Drive, and Aspects. (Note: you get the triggers associated with both of your aspects, not just one.)  
In summary, the triggers are:

- [Origin]({% link obsidian/Origins.md %}): “Did you play into the strengths and flavor of your specific Origin?”  
- [Drive]({% link obsidian/Drives.md %}): “Did you make progress on either the categorical goals of your drive, or the specific personal goals you’ve set for yourself?”  
- [Aspect(s)]({% link obsidian/Aspects.md %}): “Did you address a problem in a way that was in-line with your chosen Aspect(s)?”

#### **Inspiration**

The third, and final form of advancement is the Inspiration (INSP) track, which allows you to pursue special arcane abilities and methods. The inspiration track is a 10-long track that can be filled in a few different ways.   
	Firstly, progress on the Inspiration track can {and should\!} be granted by your GM for particularly creative or particularly team-focused solutions to problems in an Encounter. Generally, the GM should only give out 1 INSP to any player at a time with this method.   
The second is to take the \[Discover\] measure during Recess. This same measure will allow you to use the points on the Inspiration track once it’s full.   
The third only applies once the party is high enough level. If the party is level 3 or higher, a player gains 1 INSP any time *another* player fills the Experience track. 

When the inspiration track is full, using the \[Discover\] measure, a player can unlock a [Technique of the Masters]({% link obsidian/Techniques_Title_page.md %}) for the first time. When they do, they gain the GIMMICK of that technique immediately.
