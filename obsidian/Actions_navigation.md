*Misfits* is a game that gives players a lot of agency over the decisions characters can make, and thus learning to take actions can be the most difficult part of learning the game. Don’t worry though, these next sections will teach you everything you need to know. 

---
## **The Gameplay Loop**

While the details will be needed, right now the most helpful starting place is to talk about the highest level of abstraction for what playing *Misfits* looks like. 

First, the GM will describe the current environment and where the players are within it.  If you’re in a [Situation]({% link obsidian/Actions_glossary.md %}), you will have an opportunity to identify [Threats]({% link obsidian/Situations.md %}).
	Once the environment is described, the players will then take their actions, interacting with the environment and any current Threats. The players can do this in a number of different ways. If you’re in a Situation, the players can only take a limited number of actions before more Threats are presented.   
After each time a player acts, the GM will describe any results and consequences from the action, and their effects on the current environment. If you’re in \[Free Play\], the consequences of your actions may cause you to enter a Situation. If you’re in a Situation, the consequences of your actions may return you to Free Play.   
This process continues until the players choose to \[Rest\] or take [Recess]({% link obsidian/Recess.md %}).
