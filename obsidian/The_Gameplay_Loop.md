*Misfits* is a game that gives players a lot of agency over the decisions characters can make, and thus learning to take actions can be the most difficult part of learning the game. Don’t worry though, these next sections will teach you everything you need to know. 

---
## **The Gameplay Loop**

<!-- OBSIDIAN COMMENT THAT HAS BEEN REMOVED -->

Any good party has a goal they want to achieve, and any such goal is likely to have multiple--perhaps many--steps involved in achieving it.

Generally speaking, a campaign of *Misfits* will begin with the establishment of the premise and the party's ultimate goal in relation to that premise. The gameplay of any given session in misfits will then follow the following format:
* The party decides on a course of action that will bring them closer to their ultimate goal.
* The party sets out to pursue that goal. This leads to the main body of play--adventure, conflict, and so on. 
* Whether successful or not, the party retreats to a safe haven to rest, regroup, and plan their next move, all while the world continues to move around them.
* Repeat.

This format can be more formally broken down into the primary states of play: Encounters and Recess.
* *The party sets out to pursue that goal. This leads to the main body of play--adventure, conflict, and so on.*
	* Each outing makes up an [Encounter]({% link obsidian/Encounters.md %}). An Encounter encompasses everything between the party and their closest discreet goal.
	* The challenges the party meets with during this time will create one or more [Situations]({% link obsidian/Situations.md %}). When not in a Situation, the party will be in Free Play. 
* *Whether successful or not, the party retreats to a safe haven to rest, regroup, and plan their next move, all while the world continues to move around them.*
	* This leads to [Recess]({% link obsidian/Recess.md %}), where players take time to restore resources, acquire new information, allies, and equipment, and otherwise prepare for the coming challenges.
	* Once the party has finished taking Recess, the GM has the opportunity to narrate events within the world, as other factions and characters respond to the player's actions. Whether this influences the player's next course of action is a matter for their judgement, and will heavily depend on context. Whatever the case, the party is ready to gather again and head back out into the world, entering another encouter.

First, the GM will describe the current environment and where the players are within it.  If you’re in a [Situation]({% link obsidian/Situations.md %}), you will have an opportunity to identify [Threats]({% link obsidian/Situations.md %}).
	Once the environment is described, the players will then take their actions, interacting with the environment and any current Threats. The players can do this in a number of different ways. If you’re in a Situation, the players can only take a limited number of actions before more Threats are presented.   
After each time a player acts, the GM will describe any results and consequences from the action, and their effects on the current environment. If you’re in \[Free Play\], the consequences of your actions may cause you to enter a Situation. If you’re in a Situation, the consequences of your actions may return you to Free Play.   
This process continues until the players choose to \[Rest\] or take [Recess]({% link obsidian/Recess.md %}).
