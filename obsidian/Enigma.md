* ### ***Enigma ([Demigod]({% link obsidian/Demigod.md %}) \+ [Ley]({% link obsidian/Ley.md %})): \[---\]***

  * *The Technique of the Enigma*  
  * *The Technique of the*   
* The following are abilities you may take as a Enigma:  
* {E} GIMMICK: Whenever you take no actions in a round other than Preparing actions, using prepared actions, or Attuning, gain a *Paradox.*  You may expend a Paradox to exchange one of your Domains for a new one; your GM has veto power on any Domains you propose. Whenever you expend a Paradox, the next spell you cast does not require audible or somatic components.   
  * {C} ENIGMA1: When you prepare an action to cast a spell in your domain, you may expend a Paradox to ignore an augment you have the core aspect for, or to treat an augment as if you had the associated core aspect when determining costs.   
  * {C} ENIGMA2: Once between Recesses, you may Attune by spending a Paradox instead of a Focus. Attuning this way restores Focus instead of mana; restore either 1 Focus or set your Focus equal to a third of your maximum (rounded up).   
  * ~~{S} ENIGMA3: When an enemy presents a threat that targets you with a spell, you may expend a Paradox to nullify the threat.~~   
  * {E} ENIGMA4: The first time you Attune in a situation, gain a Paradox.
