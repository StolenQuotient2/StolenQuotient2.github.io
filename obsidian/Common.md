**Jumped-Up Kicks** (Support)  
A typical pair of sneakers with some enchanting decals. This item can only be bound to by someone with the Athletic Talent. Once between Recesses, when you include parkour in your action, you may forego the Athletics Proficiency reroll and instead have the action cost -1AP.

**Coward’s Compass** (Support)  
This compass always points in the exact opposite direction of your goal. When you take an action which advances you towards your goal, but in a circuitous, unintuitive, or suboptimal way, make the roll with \+1 fate. This works once between Rests.

**Blue Crystallized Mana** (Support)  
Under the right conditions, Mana can be solidified. When this happens to ley mana, it forms a blue gem, similar in appearance to sapphire. However, the crystal is brittle, and crushing the gem in your hand restores mana equal to your maximum between you and any allies you choose. <!-- OBSIDIAN COMMENT THAT HAS BEEN REMOVED -->

**Creature Feature** (Oddity)  
This small pendant with a paw embossed on the front of it does not seem to do much at first glance. However, wearing the pendant causes any cats within a large radius to meow louder and more frequently. If you stay in one place for 10 minutes or more, a house cat will come and approach you from seemingly nowhere. This item works even unbound. If you are bound to the item, the cat is particularly friendly to you.   

___
**Lucky Coin** (Support)  
At moments of decision, flipping this coin to decide on a binary choice always suggests the course that will lead to better things for the flipper. Overuse may cause the magic of this coin to burn out and revert to a normal coin.  

---
**Palaverous Podium** (Support)  
Originally created by the Dreamers for aging professors, this enchanted lectern amplifies the voice and assists the memory of those who speak while standing behind it, giving them +1 Fate for actions involving speaking to an audience.
