* ### ***Obliterator ([Demigod]({% link obsidian/Demigod.md %}) \+ [Spellblade]({% link obsidian/Spellblade.md %}))***

  * *“Slow and steady hits your face”*  
  *    
  *    
* The following are abilities you may take as an Obliterator:  
* GIMMICK: Through careful setup and precise execution, you can force your spells and attacks to hit with overwhelming force. Whenever you take an action that involves attacking with your spellblade or casting a Wreck spell, you may spend 2AP in order to perform an *Overwhelming Strike*; the action succeeds without a roll, and you cannot make attacks in the following round. Injuries dealt by Overwhelming Strikes are Severe.   
  * Cleave Through: Whenever a target is incapacitated by an attack you make, you may spend mana equal to the base cost of any spells you cast in order to inflict the same amount of damage as your original attack on another target. Targets that are incapacitated by this ability do not trigger this ability unless the original attack was an Overwhelming Strike.   
  * Everything’s a Nail: During a Situation, you may perform Overwhelming Strikes on actions that do not involve attacking with your spellblade or casting a Wreck spell. If you do, you cannot take actions in the following round.
