 Skybreaker(demigod+spellblade) so named after the mythical spear of the technique’s inventor. Master: Maeve Suggested domains: gravity, storms 


GIMMICK: Below. Plus, gain both the Blood Circle and Implicit Divinity abilities if you do not have them already. 
As a skybreaker, you can only be injured by falling if already incapacitated. You can drop safely from any height otherwise. When you take Skybreaker, you gain the ability to perform the 1ap swift action Jump. When you take the Jump action, you, well, jump. Very, very high. Think 40 stories or so of a building. You can’t use this action indoors; however, your acrobatic ability may still make movement that would be considered exerting yourself 0ap movement instead. After jumping, you must immediately take or prepare an action—preparing an action will allow you to hang in the air longer. While in the air, you are difficult to target, and preparing an action has a 1ap discount. When you take the action, prepared or not, you will crash to the ground as part of it. Plunging attacks made this way inflict severe injuries.

Storm’s Reach: augment your spellblade Blood Circle. When making an attack after jumping, a spell cast as part of that attack can originate from anywhere you could conceivably land. You do not have to physically land at that point after the attack is made. 

Elusive Jump: If you would suffer consequences contingent on hitting you physically or your presence in an area of effect, you can spend 1 focus to immediately jump out of them, avoiding the consequences altogether. You can use this jump to reposition yourself to any potential nearby vantage points, but you land without the ability to take airborne actions. You still need to pay the ap to jump, and you still need the room to jump. 

Gliding Ascent: augment your arclunge ability. When activating arclunge as part of an airbone attack, instead of a plunging attack, your angle of attack can be omnidirectional. Optionally pay 1 focus afterwards to remain airborne; this effect lasts until you take an action without activating arclunge. prerequisite: arclunge. %%
