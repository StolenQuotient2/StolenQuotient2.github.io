## **Item Tags** 

As help organizing this section, every item here will be tagged with one of the following tags:

- Weapon: A Weapon is anything created with the intent of being used as a weapon. Not every weapon is narrowly designed in a way that would prevent it from being used for other purposes.   
- Guard: A Guard is any item that protects from some kind of external stimulus, whether it be literal attacks or some other kind of effect.   
- Support: A Support is any item that assists in a task of some kind, whether it be a nonmagical task, or some kind of spellcasting assist, or some secret third thing. Anything that provides a player with a resource can also be classified as Support.   
- Oddity: An Oddity is any item that has a strange, or otherwise inexplicable effect. Oddities are often trinkets, but higher rarity Oddities can have powerful and unique properties.

## **Item Classes** 

There are a number of items which exist at a wide range of rarities. For simplicity, those items are listed here, with the differences between the rarities noted. If there is a special case of one of these items with additional properties, it will be noted as a separate item in the according rarity section. 

### ***Spellblades (Weapon)*** 

Spellblades are magical focuses and weapons combined. Spellblades come in a wide range of forms, and in a wide range of materials. Generally, they share in common that they contain a core made of a highly conductive metal or special magical material, surrounded by a more sturdy, mundane material to make it well-equipped for combat.
