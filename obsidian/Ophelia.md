The very central island of the world, Ophelia serves several purposes but is most critically the location of the marble-white castle where the God-King and his Hands reside. Neither the God-King nor the Hands often leave Ophelia, and the God-King especially stays in the throne room, except for special occasions, such as the arts and music festivals which He puts on every couple of months. Nearest to the main docks is an extraordinarily large concert venue for these events, which are free admission. The God-King has a secondary throne above the crowd for these events.   
	Ophelia receives lots of traffic even during non-event days, as the island also contains many of the offices that Church-hired enchanters and transportation mages work from, as well as a large marketplace where registered citizens can buy/sell magic items that are approved by the Church.

#### **The Hold** {#the-hold}

	The Hold is a nearly impenetrable magical prison run by \[Enforcement\], on an island off the coast of Ophelia. Koth, the Hand of Control, has worked immensely on the wards and charms that keep the prison secure. It is immune to attacks from afar, as it cannot be divined or scried, and Ophelia’s natural protections secure it from attacks in the blind. It extends an antimagical field throughout its walls and out to the very borders of the island it is on. Enforcement who have been assigned by \[NAME\], Head of The Hold can be enchanted to bypass the antimagic field. There is no other way to use magic on the island. The structure itself is highly reinforced and difficult to break through with any mundane methods. The interior layout of the prison is not well known, and is mnemonically locked to the island. Even the guards do not remember the layout while they are not on it.
