Manaseeker (Weapon)

A tiny, wasp-like construct. Once you have identified a magic item, a minor mystify spell will cause the construct to seek the nearest bearer of that item and try to sting them. The stinger can be poisoned, or primed with a spell as a means of remote delivery.

Same Day Delivery (Support)  
Send your enemies straight to God's doorstep, or anywhere else in Panops, for that matter. How this piece of paper came to be imbued with this power is unclear. When you cast Extreme level mystify spells that involve teleportation, forced movement, or other transportation of enemies or objects, you do not pay the additional Focus cost and ignore an augment of your choice when calculating mana cost.

Thirsting Knife (Weapon, Spellblade)  
This dagger grants no special combat abilities, but whatever severity of injuries is inflicted by this knife will fill an internal mana reserve 1-to-1 with mana (with a maximum of six). The wielder can spend this mana freely to cast spells or pay other mana costs.  
Showstopper (Weapon, Spellblade)  
	Showstoppers are a specialized spellblade made by the Silver Kings Metalworking Company. The process of their production is a well-kept secret by the group, but each of their more influential members carries one of the weapons. Showstoppers are easier to enchant than normal, and are generally given custom enchantments before being given to their owner. They also have a strange property called “Spellblade Dominance”; when a Showstopper wielder makes an attack against someone who is bound to a spellblade, they get \+2 Fate on that action.   
Yellow Crystallized Mana (Support)  
Under the right conditions, Mana can be solidified. When this happens to divine mana, it forms a yellow gem, similar in appearance to topaz. Unlike blue crystallized mana, yellow crystallized mana is incredibly rigid, and cannot easily be broken. If it is broken, it unleashes a destructive wave of arcane energy. Otherwise, a person with Enchantment proficiency can socket the gem into a magical artifact to increase its enchantability by 15\. If the magical artifact loses the gem somehow, it temporarily loses any enchantments it had over its normal enchantability until the crystal is reattached. 

Celestials (Support)  
A Celestial is a rare but naturally occurring gem that glows a bright color when mana is siphoned through it. Celestials are most often cut and faceted to be heart-shaped.   
While holding a Celestial, a mage may hold the gem to their chest, attempting to connect the Celestial with their soul. In order to achieve the Celestial’s full effect, the celestial needs to be moved inwards and outwards away from the chest a miniscule amount. The recommended method is for the user to take deep breaths while holding the object to their chest. Doing so takes about 2 minutes. Once the Celestial is connected with a person's soul, its effect activates and stays active for about an hour.   
While the Celestial is active, it pulses with a purple-pink glow and provides the following effects: The user gains complete immunity from emotion-altering effects. The Celestial also gives the user a surge of ambition and confidence, allowing them to attempt tasks others might deem impossible. Gain \+1 Fate whenever attempting an action of difficulty 5\. Gain an additional Fate if the action is difficulty 6 or above.   
Due to the way the Celestial responds to short bursts of mana, it is especially prized by Slipstreams; whenever you use the Slipstream GIMMICK, whether or not the Celestial is active, you may use it a second time in the same round without paying its cost.
