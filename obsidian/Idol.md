* The following are abilities you may take as an Idol:  
* GIMMICK: When you cast a spell in your domain, you may begin a *Performance*. The performance lasts until you start a new one, are incapacitated, or run out of mana. Whenever you assist on an action, you may pay the associated mana cost (in parentheses) for that performance. If you do, the ally you are assisting gains the effect listed for the rest of the round. You may also take a 1AP action and spend the associated mana cost to give yourself that effect for the rest of the round. You start with 3 Performances of your choice, with their details listed under the Songbook (Idol) ability.  
* Songbook: You may take this ability any number of times. Each time you take this ability, learn a new Performance, and/or exchange up to two of your old Performances for new ones. The list of all performances is below.   
  * *Dance Faster (2):* Actions you take that do not involve casting spells cost \-1AP. Actions you take that involve casting spells cost \+1AP.   
  * *Sing Louder (4):* You gain \+1 Fate if you have the Initiative, and \-1 Fate if you don’t.   
  * *Dream Harder (3):* You may cast Extreme level spells for \-1AP and 5 mana at base. You cannot cast Major or Minor spells.   
  * *Mage Rage (1):* Injuries you inflict are Severe.   
  * *All Eyes on Me (X):* All spells must target you. You are resistant to spells cast by enemies. (X: This performance costs 2 mana, plus an additional 2 mana for each person currently under its effect.)

* Reverberate: Whenever you take a Whim roll and your dice roll a *Full House* (Three of one number, Two of another), score 5 for that Whim Roll. Then, you may give the effect of your current Performance to an ally, if you have one. If you don’t, or can’t, instead restore 2 mana to an ally.   
* Come Out Singing: WWhen you start a Performance, you may give an ally the effect of the performance for the rest of the round. You do not have to pay the cost for this instance of the Performance’s effect.
