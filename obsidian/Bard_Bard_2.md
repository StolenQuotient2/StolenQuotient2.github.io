* ### ***Persona ([Bard]({% link obsidian/Bard.md %}) \+ Bard) \[\]***

  * *“Oh, you’re approaching me?”*  
  * *The Technique of the Persona*  
  * *The Technique of the Persona is forbidden by the Council and the Church*  
* The following are abilities you may take as a Persona:  
* GIMMICK: Choose an Origin besides Bard. Create a new character with the chosen Origin. This character is your *Persona*. The Persona shares Mana and Focus with you, as well as any abilities you have from the Persona technique, but nothing else. If your Persona would die, you do as well.   
* When you gain an ability, you may choose to either take it yourself, or to give it to your Persona. Your Persona takes abilities from their Origin, or from the Persona technique, which they are considered as having (and thus cannot take another Technique).   
* For 1AP or 1 Focus, you may *Assume your Persona*: Act as your Persona instead of yourself until you choose to switch back. Describe how they look and act, and what it looks like when they appear. If you Assume your Persona for 1 Focus instead of 1AP, they gain a temporary ability of your choice from your Persona’s origin. That ability lasts until the end of the round, or until you stop Assuming your Persona, whichever comes first.
