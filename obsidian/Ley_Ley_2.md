* ### ***Coil ([Ley]({% link obsidian/Ley.md %}) \+ Ley) \[\]*** 

  * *“Quote”*  
  * *The Technique of the Coil*  
  * *The Technique of the Coil is forbidden by the Dreamers and the Church.*   
* The following are abilities you may take as a Coil:  
* GIMMICK: You can have any amount of mana exceeding your maximum mana. While exceeding your maximum mana, you cannot Attune or restore mana passively through methods such as taking Recess. You do not lose mana over your maximum during Recess.    
* Electrify: When you assist an ally, you may spend any amount of mana. The ally gets 150% of the mana you spent, but loses any remaining after this action.   
* Teravolt Discharge: When you take an action, you may spend any amount of mana as long as you have 2 or less mana remaining afterwards. For each 4 mana you spent, gain \+1 Fate.   
* Alternating Current: When the Initiative changes, you may Attune. If you have more than your maximum mana when this happens, you may instead spend 1 Focus to gain 3 mana.
