* ### ***Summoner ([Bard]({% link obsidian/Bard.md %}) \+ [Ritualist]({% link obsidian/Ritualist.md %})) \[\]***

  * *“Quote”*  
  * *In runic, there is a single word which most students do not bother to learn, since it is long and complicated, and very hard to make a spell out of. Most attempts to create a spell from this word fail, for unknown reason. However, this word is used successfully in one specific phrase, which roughly translates to “the rabbit pulled from the hat”.*  
  * *The Technique of the Summoner is forbidden by*   
* The following are abilities you may take as a Summoner:  
* GIMMICK: You have learned to summon an otherworldly magical creature called a Starhopper. Starhoppers look vaguely similar to foxes, but they are much smaller and tend to be strange colors, such as purple or green, often with multicolor patterning. They also glow faintly. Most Starhoppers have similar dispositions to cats; Starhoppers are mischievous creatures, and they enjoy snacks, playing games, and getting into trouble. They have very quick reflexes, can move at high speeds and jump significant distances/heights. Starhoppers are also magically inclined; they have an innate sense of when magic is being cast around them, but not to the level of precision as a Ley with Third Eye or Triangulate.   
* You may summon a Starhopper for 2 Focus, and it lasts until dismissed or killed—Attuning or Recess do not dispel the Starhopper. While the Starhopper is present, it counts as a bound magical item. The Starhopper has a minor telepathic link to you, which can be strengthened when commanding the Starhopper; it counts as a sustained spell effect to use this telepathy.   
* You can command the Starhopper to perform a small task for 0AP once per round, which it carries out to the best of its ability. Anything that a small, intelligent creature could do, a Starhopper can reasonably do. This includes attacking—a Starhopper can attack others and inflict a Minor injury.  When not being actively commander, the Starhopper will make its own decisions.   
  * Little Guy Syndrome: You may take this ability any number of times. Each time you do, choose a characteristic for your Starhopper to gain. These traits are paired up–you cannot choose a trait that you already have the paired trait for.   
  * *Chatty/Shy:*   
    * Chatty: Whenever you are in non-hostile conversation, the Starhopper increases your effective level by 2, and regularly interjects with Starhopper noises.   
    * Shy: The Starhopper is exceptionally good at hiding, and can quickly obscure itself in almost any environment, despite its strange coloration and light-emission  
  * *Dutiful/Sleepy:*   
    * Dutiful: Your Starhopper provides an extra 3 Beats during Recess, provided that these Beats are used for Measures the Starhopper can participate in.   
    * Sleepy: Your Starhopper doubles your normal restoration at the start of Recess (e.g. you get 2 Focus, and restore 2 injury levels)  
  * *Independant/Loyal:*   
    * Independent: Your Starhopper acts in an encounter even if they aren't being actively commanded. Your Starhopper will always try to take actions it believes are benefitting the party, for better or worse.   
    * Loyal: Your Starhopper doesn’t count as a bound magical item. Your Starhopper can locate you easily, and attempts to return to you if lost.   
  * *Ferocious/Sharp:*   
    * Ferocious: Your Starhopper can attack twice when you command it to.   
    * Sharp: Your Starhopper's attacks inflict Severe injuries.  
  * *Clever/Single-minded*  
    * Clever: Your Starhopper understands advanced commands, and can execute a single command over multiple rounds if need be.   
    * Single-minded: Your Starhopper can reroll Whim rolls once while executing commands.   
  * *Fluffy/Lithe:*   
    * Fluffy: Your Starhopper is resistant to all magical effects.   
    * Lithe: Your Starhopper can more effectively pierce magical effects.
