**Bards** are mages who have developed a magical connection with those around them. Those who investigate this connection often find themselves able to control the fragments of divine magic in the air that have heard their name. In other words, Bards channel the power of fame.

Bards start with the following:
- 8 maximum mana
- 4 maximum Focus
- The *Silver-Tongued* [Talent]({% link obsidian/Talents.md %}). 
- The *Harmony* ability. 
- Another ability or Talent of your choice. 

---

## Bard Abilities:

- **Harmony (Starting Ability)**: You can only cast spells that affect people or target them directly. Whenever you cast a spell that doesn't inflict injuries, you may spend 1 Focus to gain 1 Fate.  

- **Charm**: Once between Recesses, you may exert control over a person’s perceptions of you and those around you. Those who are hostile to you may be made neutral, those who are neutral may be made friendly, and those who are friendly to you may become unflinchingly loyal. This effect lasts a short time, up to a minute. If you are in a Situation, spend 2 mana or 1 Focus each round this effect is active after the first.   

- **Pull Some Strings**: You have an implicit understanding of the emotional state of anyone you spend even a brief moment talking to. Once per round, for 0AP, you can get a more detailed understanding of the intentions and motives of a specific person you can see, and gain details of any Threats that they are presenting in the current round. 

- **Name Drop**: You are especially recognizable, and are beloved by many. Work with your GM to decide the nature of your fame. In populated areas, you can easily find people who know who you are, and will perform small tasks at your behest. Moreover, during Recess, gain beats equal to the number of Bard abilities you have; these beats can only be spent on tasks your followers can perform on your behalf. *{“I’ll have it for free–I’m an influencer <3”}*  

- **Polyphony**: You may sustain any number of ongoing spell effects, as long as each spell affects a different set of targets. {*In mathematics, specifically probability theory, we would say the spells are "mutually singular".*}

- **Miracle**: When you Heal an ally, you may remove the Severely Injured status from them in addition to the effects of healing. Once between Recesses, if an ally would die from an injury, you may spend 1 Focus to perform a Healing action on the ally before the injury takes effect. 

- **Deuteragonist**: Once between Recesses, when you Assist on an action, either you or the ally you are assisting may ignore the cost of Amplifying one Augment

- **Jack of All Trades**: When you take an action while not in a Situation, you may spend 2 mana to gain +1 Fate.
