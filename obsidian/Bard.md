**Bards** are mages who have developed a magical connection with those around them. Those who investigate this connection often find themselves able to control the fragments of divine magic in the air that have heard their name. In other words, Bards channel the power of fame.

Bards start with the following:
- 8 maximum mana
- 4 maximum Focus
- The *Silver-Tongued* [Talent]({% link obsidian/Talents.md %}). 
- The *Harmony* ability. 
- Another ability or Talent of your choice. 

---

## Bard Abilities:

- **Harmony (Starting Ability)**: You can only cast spells that affect people or target them directly. Whenever you cast a spell that doesn't inflict injuries, you may spend 1 Focus to gain 1 Fate.  

- **Charm**: Once between Recesses, you may exert control over a person’s perceptions of you and those around you. Those who are hostile to you may be made neutral, those who are neutral may be made friendly, and those who are friendly to you may become unflinchingly loyal. This effect lasts a short time, up to a minute. If you are in a Situation, spend 2 mana or 1 Focus each round this effect is active after the first.   

- **Pull Some Strings**: You have an implicit understanding of the emotional state of anyone you spend even a brief moment talking to. Once per round, for 0AP, you can get a more detailed understanding of the intentions and motives of a specific person you can see, and gain details of any Threats that they are presenting in the current round. 

- **Name Drop**: You are especially recognizable, and are beloved by many. Work with your GM to decide the nature of your fame. In populated areas, you can easily find people who know who you are, and will perform small tasks at your behest. Moreover, during Recess, gain beats equal to the number of Bard abilities you have; these beats can only be spent on tasks your followers can perform on your behalf. *{“I’ll have it for free–I’m an influencer <3”}*  

- **Polyphony**: You may sustain any number of ongoing spell effects, as long as each spell affects entirely different sets of targets. {*In mathematics, specifically probability theory, we would say the spells are "mutually singular" since they only exist on disjoint sets.*}

- **Miracle**: When you Heal an ally, you may remove the Severely Injured status from them in addition to the effects of healing. Once between Recesses, if an ally would die from an injury, you may spend 1 Focus to perform a Healing action on the ally before the injury takes effect. 

- **Jack of All Trades**: When you take an action while not in a Situation, you may spend 2 mana to gain +1 Fate.

- **Reverberate**: Spells you cast with unaugmented Duration persist for two turns after you take another action. Spells you cast with Amplified Duration persist for one turn after you take another action.

## Spark (Bard B-Side)

{This section is for an alternate version of the Bard origin, to be used in Alpha/Beta testing.}

Start with: 8 mana, 4 Focus, the *Quickshot* Talent, the Snap Off ability, and another ability or talent of your choice. 

- **Snap Off (Starting Ability):** Your magic flashes into existence in sudden and spectacular displays. When casting spells, ignore the *Snap* and *Extend* augments when calculating cost and potency (abilities such as Quickshot (Talent) still function). You can only cast spells with instantaneous effects, or effects that last for the round. Whenever you take an action that is performed quickly or recklessly, you may spend 1 Focus to gain 1 Fate. 
  
- **Cascade**: When you cast a spell that succeeds on its action by 2 or more, you may cast the spell again as a 1AP Swift Action on the next round. 

- **Arpeggiate:** When you cast a spell, you may cast another spell, as long as you can pay the mana cost for both. Casting spells in this way causes them to fire off in sequence, rather than all at once. 

- **Shine Bright (formerly: Name Drop):** You've developed a reputation for your style of spellcasting, both in the types of spells you choose and the way you carry yourself while casting. When you cast a spell in a populated area, you are likely to find people who recognize you from your spellcasting, and who will offer you information for the low cost of talking about your magic with them for a few minutes. Moreover, during Recess, gain beats equal to the number of Spark abilities you have; these beats can only be spent to gather information from people, or to put yourself in contact with magical parties. 

- **Inspire (formerly Pull Some Strings):** You have found a faint magical connection between yourself and those of your closest allies. When you participate in an Entwined action with a member of your party, up to one person of your choice may reroll their Whim roll before applying Fate. This effect allows for multiple rerolls, even if a player has a Talent that already grants them a reroll.  

- **Fission**: When you remove a Condition from an ally, that ally gains 2 mana for each level of the removed Condition. 

- **Fuse**: You may cast Extreme spells as 3AP Swift Actions. If you do, they activate on the *following* round.
