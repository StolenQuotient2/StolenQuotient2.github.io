Below is a list of creatures that you can use as encounters or enemies during Misfits. Every non-player animal or person is considered a creature, and you will find categories below for humanoid enemies in addition to typical fauna.   

All creatures have three extra traits to help determine their usefulness in certain encounters: their Intelligence, their Magic, and their Threat Level.   

A creature’s Threat Level ranges from Very Low to Very High. Very Low threat enemies are good matches 1st-2nd level players, and often appear in groups, and Very High threat enemies are often challenges for even 5th level players, and tend to appear alone.   

A creature’s intelligence can be Wild, Intelligent or Sapient. Wild creatures rely on instinct to make their actions, Intelligent creatures can understand language and respond to basic commands, and Sapient creatures are on the level of humans–full understanding of language, problem solving, etc.   
A creature’s Magic can either be nonexistent (Mundane), they can be a Caster or they can be a Weaver. A Caster is any magic user that does not follow the same spellcasting system as the players. Often Casters will be restricted to specific kinds of spells, or very specific magical effects. Weavers are effectively NPC player characters, utilizing the same methods of casting and wielding the same abilities as players can.

#### **Squirrels**

Health: 1  
Intelligence: Wild  
Magic: Caster  
Threat: Varies  
	While there are mundane squirrels, a strange oddity about many squirrels is that they will discover how to cast spells on accident. Particularly clever squirrels will learn to repeat that series of sounds and somatics they made in order to repeat this.   
	Squirrels learn a single spell and can cast it any number of times each encounter. The spell can be of any potency and have up to a single augment.   
	The threat of a squirrel depends entirely on what spell it knows. A mundane squirrel is a very low threat. Squirrels with higher potency wreck spells can be remarkably dangerous. {“Squirrel uses Hyper Beam\!”} A squirrel that made it into \[Ophelia\] once caused overwhelming havoc by casting the spell “teleport another squirrel to my position”. \[Enforcement\] was dispatched, and suffered many casualties.   
	Squirrels tend not to interfere with humans unless provoked, and generally avoid confrontation afterwards. If backed into a corner, squirrels will fight with impressive vitality.

#### **Wyvern**

Health: 6  
Intelligence: Sapient  
Magic: Caster  
Threat: High  
While now near-extinct in the wild, Wyverns are a popular choice for Church-created dungeons. Large, flying, sentient magic users make for a wide variety of interesting characters. Bosses or helpful lorekeepers, mounts or looming threats. Wyverns have gold-white scales and long snaking bodies.   
Wyverns may cast as many Minor or Major potency spells as they like, with any number of augments.  
Wyvern scales are a mana-absorbent material, and after a Wyvern has received 50 mana’s worth of spells from sources other than itself, it can choose to release that mana in a destructive burst, equivalent to an Extreme level wreck spell. A wyvern can attempt to counter a spell by absorbing it up to Once between Recesses, and any spell the wyvern counters in this way counts for double the normal mana.   
Wyverns fight by flying high above their combatants, and raining down spells on top of them. They stay out of the way of mundane attacks and use their natural magic absorption to channel their destructive burst before diving towards their targets and releasing their charged mana.
