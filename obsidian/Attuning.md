## **Attuning**

When you run out of mana, you will no longer be able to cast spells. However, you may choose to Attune in the middle of play to restore some mana. You may Attune for either 0AP or 1AP. In either case, spend 1 Focus. A 0AP Attune is called a *Quick-Attune*, a 1AP Attune is called a *Full-Attune*.   
For a Quick-Attune, make a Whim roll. Restore mana equal to double your score. If you scored 0, restore 1 mana instead. Attuning in this way does not break sustained spell effects.   
For a Full-Attune, restore all missing mana.
