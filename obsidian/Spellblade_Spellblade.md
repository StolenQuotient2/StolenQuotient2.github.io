* ### ***Bladestorm ([Spellblade]({% link obsidian/Spellblade.md %}) \+ Spellblade): \[\]***

  * *The Technique of the Bladestorm*   
  * *The Technique of the*   
* The following are abilities you may take as a Bladestorm:  
* GIMMICK: Injuries inflicted by attacks you make with your spellblade are Minor. Whenever you succeed on an action where you inflict an injury with your spellblade, you may attempt the action again. If you succeed, inflict another injury of the same type to the same target or a nearby target, and you may repeat the process. If you fail, apply only injuries from the first roll and ignore the effects of any subsequent rolls.   
* Feint: The first time you fail a roll while using your GIMMICK, you may pay 1 Focus, ignore the failure, and roll again.
