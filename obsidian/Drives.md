You didn’t just start a Reaper, you joined for a reason. Pick a category that your reason falls into; the specifics are up to you and your GM.

Each drive gives the same mechanical benefits—focus on the details that are important to your character when making your choice. 

---

### ***Revolution:*** 

* You believe in the Reapers’ cause: to stretch the limits of magic, to remove the barriers put in place by the Church, the Council, and the Dreamers, and to build your place in the world.   
	* Work with your GM to describe the mischief you’ve already found yourself in with the Reapers. Who have you messed with, and are they out to get you? How many times have you caused problems for this group of people, and are you planning to cause even more?  
	* Pick a personal goal for your character. How does this goal fall under Revolution? What do you need to do to achieve it?  
	* Pick a [Talent]({% link obsidian/Talents.md %}) you feel represents your character’s background, and pick a second Talent you feel represents the specific details of your Drive. Gain both of these Talents. 

### ***Insight:***

* You believe that the Reapers’ are the key to unlocking the secrets of magic. You want the hands on experience with magic in order to progress your studies, and perhaps even add to the advancement of arcane knowledge.   
  * Work with your GM to describe the areas of magic you’re interested in, and the reason you’re going to the Reapers instead of the local library. What is weird, dangerous, or taboo about the magic you want to study and the way you want to study it?  
  * Pick a personal goal for your character. How does this goal fall under Insight? What do you need to do to achieve it?  
  * Pick a [Talent]({% link obsidian/Talents.md %}) you feel represents your character’s background, and pick a second Talent you feel represents the specific details of your Drive. Gain both of these Talents. 

### ***Survival:***  

* Maybe something terrible happened to you. Perhaps someone is after you. Whatever it is, the Reapers are protecting you and providing you with the space and knowledge to protect yourself in the future.   
	* Work with your GM to describe the trauma your character is running from. What people, places, or otherworldly forces combined to force you into hiding? How do you plan to deal with those situations?  
	* Pick a personal goal for your character. How does this goal fall under Survival? What do you need to do to achieve it?  
	* Pick a [Talent]({% link obsidian/Talents.md %}) you feel represents your character’s background, and pick a second Talent you feel represents the specific details of your Drive. Gain both of these Talents.   


### ***Kinship:***  

* The Reapers are full of some really cool people, and you joined to be closer to those people. The “being the most powerful mages in the world” thing is cool also.   
	* Work with your GM to describe the people you know who convinced you to join the Reapers. Were you friends with other party members? Maybe one of the \[Important NPCs\]? How much do you trust them and their plans?  
	* Pick a personal goal for your character. How does this goal fall under Kinship? What do you need to do to achieve it?  
	* Pick a [Talent]({% link obsidian/Talents.md %}) you feel represents your character’s background, and pick a second Talent you feel represents the specific details of your Drive. Gain both of these Talents. 

### ***Sanctuary:*** 

* You've seen enough suffering in Panops, and you've decided to do something about it. You joined the Reapers to help people, drawn by the despairing and the downtrodden left in the wake of the Drain and the Council's stranglehold.  
	* Work with your GM to describe the people you're looking to help, and why they matter to you. What have you seen that impelled you to join the Reapers? Did you lose someone important to you?  
	* Pick a personal goal for your character. How does this goal fall under Sanctuary? What do you need to do to achieve it?  
	* Pick a [Talent]({% link obsidian/Talents.md %}) you feel represents your character’s background, and pick a second Talent you feel represents the specific details of your Drive. Gain both of these Talents.
