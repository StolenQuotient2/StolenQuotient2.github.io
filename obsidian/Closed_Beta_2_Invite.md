Hello! If you're reading this (and not snooping around in the github files) then you're one of the people I am inviting to join in Misfits' Closed Beta. ### Option 1: Circling the Drain.

Everyone has met Edie. She's a bubbly and youthful (read: she's about 12-13) mainstay of Alpha HQ and has been there longer than most Reapers. She also has one of the most severe cases of The Drain ever seen. Those who can detect magical ability can tell that Edie would be a Demigod, if not for the Drain tapping her power. Most days, she's perfectly fine, though sometimes she prefers to use a wheelchair since the drain makes it tiring to walk. 

However, as of recently, Edie has started feeling worse and worse--the Drain is accelerating, somehow, and she is slowly deteriorating. Your group is dedicated to finding out what is going on; seeking a reason, and hopefully a cure, for Edie's case of the Drain. 

The campaign would circulate around exploring the history and effects of the Drain, and exploring the world and talking with mages who have tried to heal it before. This campaign will be fairly investigative, as you develop a deeper understanding of the Drain and its cultural context. There will nonetheless be plenty of opportunities to blow things up. 


### Option 2: A Right to Remain Silenced

The Council uses many excuses to become a more unjust and corrupt organization. In particular, they like to blame the Reapers for everything. The Council has used the "threat" of the Reapers in order to justify massively increasing their presence and quantity of arrests in many of the cities they occupy, turning to more acquisitions of peoples' belongings and thus more money for the faction as a whole. 

Recently, a Council building in Wei-Jin was collapsed due to a localized earthquake spell. The perpetrators also killed and hid the body of Horace Waterfield, a high-ranking detective with a sterling reputation among the Council. Despite not having any substantial evidence, the Council blames the Reapers (which is, unfortunately, a correct allegation). Like a switch being flipped, the Council have gone from activities that can be excused as "simply protecting the city" to targeting Reapers in broad daylight. 

Interestingly, however, several high-rank Council members have made statements publicly condemning these recent activities, and have taken actions to punish Council members engaging in them. Moreover, Reapers who have found themselves attacked by the Council will sometimes note a strange behavior or appearance of these Council members—glowing eyes, inhuman speed, and an uncanny aura of intimidation. It is clear that this is not simply an escalation of the Council's corruption--its something far more sinister. 

This campaign will circulate around discovering the real reason for the Council attacks, and will be much more combat oriented. What investigation you have to do will be behind locked doors and sealed vaults, and you will have to either sneak or fight your way through the streets of Wei-Jin in order to reach these places due to the public Council presence.
