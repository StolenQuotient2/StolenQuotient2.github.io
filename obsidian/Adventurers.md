Various people running through the dungeons in Sheol. Below are some particularly influential past/present adventuring teams. 

#### **The Evokers** 

The Evokers were the first team to ever win the yearly games, and did so by such a large margin that they are largely considered the best team to ever play. Now retired, they live in lavish mansions on the Kaelo Archipelago.

#### **The Legends** 

The Legends named themself before they managed to get 2nd place in four consecutive seasons of the games. They’re on track to do so a fifth time. 

#### **The Silent** 

The Silent do their best to be completely anonymous. However, they are so effective in the games that many people can’t help but speculate who this group is. Some claim that the Silent are made up of retired adventurers, such as Evokers who want to get back into the games. Others claim that the Silent is a large group of relatively low-level adventurers, who use sheer numbers to rise to the top.
