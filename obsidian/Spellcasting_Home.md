Spellcasting is the heart and soul of Misfits; much of your time spent in the game will be crafting spells on the fly in order to adapt to whatever situations your GM throws at you. This section will describe the qualitative system that Misfits uses to build spells. 

Every spell begins with a verbal description of the spell effect. There are many ways to describe a spell that achieves the same goal (See \[Spell Examples\]) and the description of your spell will determine its cost through which \[Augments\] your description applies to. 

In the world of Panops, a mage casting a spell needs to use both an Audible and Somatic component, meaning that the mage must say something, or make a distinct sound, and also make a gesture with their hands or other objects. 

---

## Requirements of Spellcasting

In order to cast a spell, you must have the following:
- A *Somatic* Component
- An *Audible* Component
- Sufficient Mana

A Somatic component is a physical motion you make, almost always with your hands or arms. An Audible component is a sound, but it need not be a set of spoken words. Having sufficient mana depends on the spell being cast, and will be the subject of the rest of this section. 

In addition, a spell cannot be cast if it violates the rules of [The Veil]({% link obsidian/The_Veil.md %}). 

## Mana Cost, Augments, and Potency.

Every spell costs 2 mana at base, and must cost at least 1 mana after all modifications from all sources. This number is increased for each *Augment* that you apply. When you apply an Augment, increase the cost of the spell by 2. Furthermore, each of the 8 Augments is paired up (visually, they are in the same row of the table below). If you apply both augments in a row, double the mana cost of the spell. 

Each augment is indicated to either *Diminish* or *Amplify* the spell. A spell is determined to be Minor, Major or Extreme based on if it has more Diminishes overall or more Amplifications overall. A spell with more Diminishes is Minor. A spell with more Amplifications is Extreme. A spell with a balanced amount is Major. 

Below is a table of all of the augments and the conditions where they apply. The "Standard" column indicates the description of a spell that is not augmented by either augment in that row. Furthermore, Amplifying and Diminishing the same category allows you to "pick and choose" from those augments' effects. For example, augmenting a spell with both Snap and Extend would allow you to cast a Sustained spell extremely quickly.

### *Giving 110%*

Some spells take your all, and then some. Giving 110% is a way to push the extra sparks of mana into a spell that will otherwise drain you. When you cast a spell that would cost more than your remaining mana, check to see if you would be able to cast the spell with extra mana equal to one-tenth of your maximum mana (rounded up). If you would, you may cast the spell regardless of the mana cost, and set your current mana to zero.

### **Sustained Spell Effects** 

Spells that have amplified Duration are called *Sustained* spells. These are spells that have been imbued with enough mana to not need continual upkeep to maintain their effect. By default, a caster can only sustain one spell at a time. Certain special abilities (e.g. Polyphony (Bard)) can modify this number. 

Sustained spells can be dispelled through a number of means. Here are the possible conditions. The *controller* of the spell is the caster by default, but if a spell is hijacked in some way by another mage, this may change.

- The controller chooses to end the effect.  
- The stated duration of the spell lapses.   
- The controller \[Full-Attunes\].   
- The spell is \[Countered\] in some way by another mage. There are a few ways of doing this, which are described in \[The Grand Theory of Magic\].   
- The controller is incapacitated or killed.
### **Attuning**
When you run out of mana, you will no longer be able to cast spells. However, you may choose to Attune in the middle of play to restore some mana. You may Attune for either 0AP or 1AP. In either case, spend 1 Focus. A 0AP Attune is called a *Quick-Attune*, a 1AP Attune is called a *Full-Attune*.   
For a Quick-Attune, make a Whim roll. Gain mana equal to double your score. If you scored 0, gain 1 mana instead. Attuning in this way allows you to temporarily exceed your maximum mana, and does not break sustained spell effects.  
For a Full-Attune, restore all missing mana. 

### *Resolving Spell Interpretations*
The GM and players should work together to make sure that any spells cast have reasonable interpretations in the world, even if sometimes there are unintended side effects (which may take the form of consequences of particular actions). However, given the qualitative nature of spellcasting in *Misfits*, here are a few extra guidelines to base your interpretations on.  
Firstly, consider the narrative. Ultimately spells are a tool to serve the story  
you are choosing to tell, and you may find that one spell interpretation much better serves the narrative than the other.   
Secondly, look at \[The Grand Theory of Magic\]. This section describes in detail the nature of mana and spellcasting in Panops, and often will provide answers to many conflicts. In particular, \[Spell Morphology\] may be of use. 
### ***Optional: Draincasting Spells***
*This is an Optional Rule. Ask your GM if it is being used.*   
As a last-ditch effort, mages can draw upon the life-sustaining mana of their own soul in order to cast spells. Doing so costs 1 Focus, and no mana, but inflicts a Severe injury equal to the potency of the cast spell, plus an additional severe minor injury for every Amplification. You may also Draincast without spending Focus, but it inflicts an additional injury equal to the potency of the spell.
