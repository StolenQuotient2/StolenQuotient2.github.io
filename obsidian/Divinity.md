When the God-King created Panops, he was at a height of Divine power that is now unattainable. Since He had no worshippers, the power granted to Him while He was creating Panops slowly left him and leaked into the world. This gives us the byproduct of Demigods and Bards.   
	Divine mana itself has some strange properties. It is incredibly potent; a small amount of Divine mana can produce the same magical effects as a much larger amount of Ley mana. More importantly, Divine mana has “memory”, in a sense. Rather than returning to a leyline after being dispersed into the air, Divine mana returns to its “owner”. 

### ***Demigods***

A Demigod is a divine magic user whose divinity is innate, in some sense. All souls contain divine magic, but sometimes a soul captures some of the lingering divine mana in the world before being born. This allows them to draw from the power of their soul to cast spells and use other magical abilities. Since the power of Demigods is intrinsically tied to their personhood, the type of person they are dictates their mana, resulting in the various Domains that demigods have. 

### ***Bards***

A Bard is a divine magic user who gained their divinity. Bards are about as close as you can be to Becoming a god. Bards’ divine magic is, as opposed to Demigods, drawn from the souls of others. Fame, Worship, etc. towards a specific person disperses a very small (read: negligible) amount of Divine mana from the worshiper’s soul into the air, and assigns the owner of that mana to the Bard. The more worship or fame that a Bard has, the more powerful they become.
