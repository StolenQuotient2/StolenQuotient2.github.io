## **Goals and Adventure Sheets** 

The Goal of your party is a framework to help assist with the structure of your campaign. Unlike a Drive, your party’s Goal represents an overarching connection between all of the players. Why does your group stick together? Your Goal is one reason why.   
In addition, each of the three goals associates you with a specific Adventure Sheet, which will serve as the central tracking of your advancement through the campaign.  
	Each Goal (listed below) has a number of questions designed to help build a unique story based off of your characters’ individual personalities and wishes.   
***Uprising***

- Join the Reapers’ frontline in fighting against the Dreamers, the Council, and the Church. Disrupt and dismantle the oppositions’ plans and fight for your place in the world.   
  - What group, person, or plan is your team’s main target? What set-up do you need to defeat this target–do they have any specific weaknesses to exploit, or strengths you will need to circumvent?   
  - Why does your group care about this target in specific? What history does your character have with this target?   
- *Suggested Milestones:*  
  - Removing wards or protections from your primary target. Defeating high-profile targets. Acquiring a powerful tool or weapon. Destruction of important resources or locations. Gaining key intel about your target(s). 

***Enlightenment***

- Seek the secrets of the arcane. Find new and exciting ways to use magic, and then utilize them to their greatest potential.   
  - What magical artifact(s) or technique(s) are so interesting to the party? Why is this arcane force hard to find, learn or use? Who do you need to seek out along the way in order to get access to it?   
  - What purpose does your group have with this magic? What goals does your character have that requires the use of this artifact or technique?    
- *Suggested Milestones:*  
  - Gaining a primary component of an item you are building. Meeting a mage who can teach you a specific technique(s). Finding archaic source(s) of information relevant to you. 

***Competition***

- Panops is home to a number of different games and tournaments, and you’re GONNA BE THE VERY BEST, LIKE NO-ONE—huh? Oh, sorry, my lawyer is telling me we can be sued for that.  
  - What game do you plan on competing in? Spellskating? Competitive adventuring? Some secret third thing? {Calvinball?}  
  - What is your goal by participating in this activity? Is there some big tournament to win? Are you competing for fame, or fortune? Is there some specific magical object that you want to get as a prize? Is there a specific person you just really want to rub your victory in the face of?  
- *Suggested Milestones:*  
  - Winning matches. Acquiring new skills or equipment. Meeting a master of the sport who can train you. Defeating a longstanding rival.
