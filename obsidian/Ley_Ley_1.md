### ***Awakened ([Ley]({% link obsidian/Ley.md %}) \+ Ley) \[\]*** 

- **Sparkmind (Starting Ability):** You have exceptional awareness and command of the mana in the air around you. Whenever you Attune, you make yourself aware of the patterns of mana in the surrounding area; until you next cast a spell, actions you take that interact with mana or spellcasting have +1 Fate. 

- **Alight**: By manipulating currents of mana in the air, you can create temporary forces which allow you to hover and fly short distances. Spend 2 mana each time you start to do this. You can move as fast as you would while running, and can fly as high as a 1 or 2 story building. When you are in an area of particularly high leyline activity, you can fly much faster and much higher. 

- **Static**: As a Swift Action, you may spend 1 AP and 1 Focus to render the mana in the near vicinity inert for a short period of time. While this effect is active, noone in your vicinity can cast spells, including you. Each turn, you may spend 1AP to keep the effect ongoing. Otherwise, the effect ends. 

- **Maelstrom**: One between recesses, you may command the mana in the air to form a thunderous and swirling storm, called a *Maelstrom*. The Maelstrom is centered on yourself, and moves with you while you walk or run. If you have Alight (Awakened), you may also fly around within the storm for free. Teleporting or moving at extreme speeds causes the storm to disperse. When you or an ally Quick-Attunes within the Maelstrom, they may score Arcana on their Whim rolls. Furthermore, if that ally has Deepest Reserves (Ley), they may gain the mana from Quick-Attuning as if they did not have the Deepest Reserves (Ley) ability. When an enemy casts a spell within the Maelstrom, you may spend 3 mana and 1 AP to blast them with a bolt of mana, which inflicts a Major injury.
