The Dreamers are the core educational body in Panops, and operates the vast majority of the schools in the world. The Dreamers have both nonmagical and magical schools. Moreover, the Dreamers operate universities and research institutions for a variety of subjects in higher education, and Dreamer universities produce the most Enchanters of any institution.
