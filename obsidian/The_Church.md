The Church is the overall governing body in Panops. Loyal to and overseen by the God-King, the Church is in charge of a number of important projects and programs, and it operates as the supreme justice of the world. The Church oversees the Arcane and Mundane Transit Authority, or \[AMTA\], which provides free or cheap transportation to nearly every corner of Panops. It also builds, and stocks the dungeons of \[Sheol\], for adventurers of all kinds to test their mettle. The Church also runs a number of \[job seeking/homeless shelters\]
