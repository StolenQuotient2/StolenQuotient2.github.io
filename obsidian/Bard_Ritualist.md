*In Runic, there is a single word which most students do not bother to learn, since it is long and complicated, and very hard to make a spell out of. Most attempts to create a spell from this word fail, for unknown reason. However, this word is used successfully in one specific phrase, which roughly translates to “the rabbit pulled from the hat”.*

## **Summoner ([Bard]({% link obsidian/Bard.md %}) \+ [Ritualist]({% link obsidian/Ritualist.md %})) 

- **SUMMONER1 (Starting Ability)**: When you take SUMMONER1, lose the Harmony (Bard) and Scrivener (Ritualist) abilities, and gain the Starhopping (Summoner) ability. Whenever you take an action that involves summoning or commanding creatures or people, you may spend 1 Focus to gain 1 Fate. 

- **Starhopping:** You have learned to summon an otherworldly magical creature called a *Starhopper*. Starhoppers look vaguely similar to foxes, but they are much smaller and tend to be strange colors, such as purple or green, often with multicolor patterning. They also glow faintly. Most Starhoppers have similar dispositions to cats; Starhoppers are mischievous creatures, and they enjoy snacks, playing games, and getting into trouble. They have very quick reflexes, can move at high speeds and jump significant distances/heights. Starhoppers are also magically inclined; they have an innate sense of when magic is being cast around them, but not to the level of precision as mage with Third Eye (Ley).
	- You may summon a Starhopper for 2 Focus, and it lasts until dismissed or killed—Attuning or Recess do not dispel the Starhopper. Starhoppers are killed when they reach the Extreme injury level (as opposed to players, who need to reach the Dead injury level). While the Starhopper is present, it counts as a bound magical item. The Starhopper has a minor telepathic link to you, which can be strengthened when commanding the Starhopper; while you are maintaining this stronger telepathy, it counts as a sustained spell effect.   
	- You can command the Starhopper to perform a small task for 0AP once per round as a Swift action, which it carries out to the best of its ability. Anything that a small, intelligent creature could do, a Starhopper can reasonably do. This includes attacking—a Starhopper can attack others and inflict a Minor injury.  When not being actively commanded, the Starhopper will make its own decisions. Most actions a Starhopper takes will be Swift. 

- **Little Guy Syndrome**: You may take this ability any number of times. Each time you do, choose a characteristic for your Starhopper to gain. These traits are paired up–you cannot choose a trait that you already have the paired trait for.   
	- *Chatty/Shy:*   
	    * Chatty: Whenever you are in non-hostile conversation, your Starhopper grants you +1 Fate, and regularly interjects with Starhopper noises.   
	    * Shy: Your Starhopper is exceptionally good at hiding, and can quickly obscure itself in almost any environment, despite its strange coloration and light-emission  
	  * *Dutiful/Sleepy:*   
	    * Dutiful: Your Starhopper provides an extra 3 Beats during Recess, provided that these Beats are used for Measures the Starhopper can participate in.   
	    * Sleepy: Your Starhopper doubles your normal restoration at the start of Recess (e.g. you get 2 Focus, and restore 2 injury levels)  
	  * *Independant/Loyal:*   
	    * Independent: Your Starhopper acts in an encounter even if they aren't being actively commanded. Your Starhopper will always try to take actions it believes are benefitting the party, for better or worse.   
	    * Loyal: Your Starhopper doesn’t count as a bound magical item. Your Starhopper can locate you easily, and attempts to return to you if lost. Your Starhopper is more resistant to effects that would cause it to betray you or ignore your commands.    
	  * *Ferocious/Sharp:*   
	    * Ferocious: Your Starhopper can attack twice when you command it to.   
	    * Sharp: Your Starhopper's attacks inflict Severe injuries.  
	  * *Clever/Single-minded*  
	    * Clever: Your Starhopper understands advanced commands, and can execute a single command over multiple rounds if need be.   
	    * Single-minded: Your Starhopper can reroll Whim rolls once while executing commands.   
	  * *Fluffy/Lithe:*   
	    * Fluffy: Your Starhopper is resistant to all magical effects.   
	    * Lithe: Your Starhopper can more effectively pierce magical effects.
