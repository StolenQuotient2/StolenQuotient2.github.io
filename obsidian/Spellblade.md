 *Those without the innate ability to see leylines can still draw power from them using a specially crafted weapon, called a Spellblade (although it need not be a “blade”). Spellblades absorb latent magical energy around them, and allow for redistribution by the user. They’re both a weapon, and an arcane focus. You wield one such blade.*

**Spellblades** are named after their weapon, spellblades. These weapons are specially crafted out of materials that conduct mana well, allowing for a magically-inclined person to use the weapon to cast spells in addition to nonmagical attacks. While Spellblades do not possess as strong of an innate magical gift as other origins, they remain extraordinarily capable and powerful mages. 


Spellblades start with the following:
- 8 maximum mana
- 4 maximum Focus
- A Common Spellblade — the weapon takes whatever form you wish. It need not be a "blade".
- The *Martial* [Talent]({% link obsidian/Talents.md %}). 
- The *Blood Circle* ability. 
- Another ability or Talent of your choice. 

---
## Spellblade Abilities:

- **Blood Circle (Starting Ability)**: Your magic extends not far beyond the reach of your weapon; spells you cast can only originate from and be sustained within a sphere about 10 or 15 feet across that is centered on yourself. When you make a magical attack, you may spend 1 Focus to gain 1 Fate. {The term "blood circle" is what Boy Scouts use to describe the range of your knife while you're whittling. It is dangerous to step inside the blood circle.}

- **Arclunge**: You may spend 1 mana to create a magical impulse beneath your feet that launches you in a chosen direction. You may do this at any time, including during an action. when you make an attack with your spellblade that utilizes this impulse for momentum or positioning, injuries inflicted from that attack are Severe. 

- **Stand With Me**: Whenever an ally standing within your *Blood Circle* is targeted by an attack or spell, you may spend 3 mana and 1 AP to manifest a magical impulse that intercepts the effect. If the effect is nonmagical, or takes the form of a projectile, the effect is fully nullified. Otherwise, the ally gains +1 Fate to any actions to prevent the effect. 

- **Steadfast**: You are more resilient to injuries in general. If you are not Severely Injured, injuries you take cannot compound, and when you are Severely injured, your chance to compound injuries is reduced by 25% back to the standard 50% chance. 

- **Proxy**: *Blood Circle* also applies to a sphere centered around your spellblade, even when you are not carrying it—as long as you are certain of the spellblade's location. You may spend 2 mana at any time to instantly return your spellblade to your hand, regardless of its current location.

- **What Doesn't Kill You**: The first attack you make after being injured has +1 Fate. Injuries you inflict upon yourself as part of an action to make an attack trigger this bonus for that action.

- **Honed**: Whenever you attack with your spellblade, you may spend 1 Focus, or 2AP. If you do, the attack pierces through any nonmagical defenses and is more effective against magical ones. 

- **Cutting Edge**: You have a particular affinity and fondness for magical artifacts. You can quickly identify the name and rarity of any artifact you come across, and you gain a sense if the artifact is dangerous to handle or use. You may spend 2 mana to gain details of the workings and enchantments on a magical item you are standing next to; if the item is dangerous, you gain details of how to mitigate or avoid its danger.

- **Honor-Bound**: You have joined *The Path of Crossed Swords*, a Spellblade-only order of knights dedicated to protecting those around you, and changing the world for the better. The Path is held in very high regard across all factions—to other Spellblades, you are considered noble, and Spellblades will be much more highly inclined to aid you or listen to the things you say. During Recess, you can call upon The Path to aid you in completing measures, especially those which involve dangerous conditions or protecting your allies.

### Kirpara (Spellblade B-side)

{This section is for an alternate version of the Spellblade origin, to be used in Alpha/Beta testing.}

Start with: 4 mana, 8 Focus, A set of bespoke *Kirpara*, your choice of the *Lashweaver* or *Shieldbearer* talents, the *Weldweave* ability, and another ability or talent of your choice. 

- **Weldweave (Starting Ability):** Your Kirpara allow you to focus mana into precise, direct effects in ways that other spellcasters struggle to replicate. As a consequence, you cannot cast spells that are Augmented. When you use mana to move, attack, or protect yourself or allies, you may spend 1 Focus to gain 1 Fate. 

- **Helixwind:** Full-Attuning costs 0 AP.  

- **Forceweave:** You may spend 1 mana to create a magical impulse that forces you and an object closer together, or further apart; if the object is light, or you are anchored down, the object moves. If the object is heavy, you move. If you and the object weigh about the same, you both move. Both you and the chosen target must be on the ground for this ability to work correctly; if either you or the object is in the air, the effects of this force are chaotic and unpredictable. {Yes, alpha testers, its a Mistborn. I'm doing a Mistborn.}

- **Immovable:** You may spend 1 mana to anchor yourself to the ground using your leg bracers. While this connection is held, you cannot be moved from your present location by either magical or nonmagical means. You are unaffected by penalties from injuries in this state, and your injuries cannot compound in this state. You still possess enough range of motion to reorient yourself, and to duck or dodge within a radius of a couple feet. 

- **Constructor:** Lashes and Shields you summon from the *Lashweaver* and *Shieldbearer* talents are far more stable. You may spend mana instead of Focus to use their effects that aren't summoning. Moreover, if you run out of Focus, you may choose to persist the Lash or Shield using mana instead, meaning that they only dispel when you next run out of mana. You may take this ability twice: the second time you take this ability, gain the Lashweaver and Shieldbearer talents if you do not have them already, and when you would summon a Lash, you may summon a Shield as well for no additional cost. 

- **Blastcage**: You may spend 1 mana to protect your hands and arms and attempt to use them to deflect a weapon or projectile without being injured. If you succeed, you may spend 1 Focus to catch the weapon or projectile instead of deflecting it. If the caught item is a spell, you may throw it at a new target as a 1AP standard action.  

- **Steelstep (formerly: Arclunge)**: You may spend 1 mana to create a magical impulse beneath your feet that launches you in a chosen direction. You may do this at any time, including during an action. Those close to you when you use this effect take a minor injury if they are unprotected. The force of this impulse is enough to launch you several stories into the air if you choose. 

- **Veritable (formerly: Honor-Bound):** You have pledged the *Vero*, the oath that Kirpara take to join the Guardians. You can wear the symbol of the Guardians, and are subject to the high regard that other factions give to the Guardians as a consequence. To other Kirpara, you are considered honorable, and Kirpara will be much more highly inclined to aid you or listen to the things you say. During Recess lasting longer than a couple hours, you are expected to aid the Guardians by taking a measure for 2 Beats, but you may also call upon the Guardians at any time to aid you in completing measures, especially those which involve dangerous conditions, or protecting innocents. Furthermore, you may call for aid from the Guardians during an encounter--it is up to your GM how quickly they are able to respond.
