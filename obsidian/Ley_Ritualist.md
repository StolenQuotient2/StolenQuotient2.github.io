### Channeler ([Ley]({% link obsidian/Ley.md %}) + [Ritualist]({% link obsidian/Ritualist.md %}))

- **CHANNELER1 (Starting Ability)**: When you take CHANNELER1, gain the Deepest Reserves (Ley) and Scrivener (Ritualist) abilities if you do not have them already. If you gained the Deepest Reserves (Ley) ability in this way, double your maximum mana. Additionally, gain the Spin Up (Channeler) ability. 

- **Spin Up**: You may spend 2 AP to start a *Channel*. Activating a Channel causes a bright ring of runes to appear on the ground surrounding you. Until you take an action that ends the Channel, you cannot take Standard Actions, and cannot move outside the ring of runes. Doing either *disrupts* the Channel, ending it. Furthermore, the Channel is disrupted if you are injured, restrained, silenced, or otherwise prevented from casting spells. Each time a player takes a Standard Action while you have a Channel ongoing, spend 1 mana and add it to the ongoing Channel. When a Channel is disrupted, lose all mana stored in the Channel. You may willingly end the Channel in any of the following ways:
	- *Abort*: As a 1AP Swift Action, end the Channel and restore mana equal to the mana stored. 
	- *Accumulate*: You may Full-Attune with an ongoing Channel; when you do, end the Channel. Instead of the normal mana restoration from Full-Attuning (or lack thereof, due to Deepest Reserves (Ley)), gain mana equal to double the amount stored in the Channel. This mana allows you to temporarily exceed your maximum mana. You may Accumulate only once between Recesses.
	- *Align*: You may cast a spell with an ongoing Channel (for its standard AP cost); when you do, double the amount of mana in the Channel, and cast a spell using that mana. Any remaining mana is restored to you. Similarly, any mana you had that was not stored in the channel may be used to cast the spell, but only if you've already used all of the mana stored in the channel. Spells cast this way (in view of Scrivener (Ritualist)) can target anything you can touch from within your runic circle, or anything in contact with the surface the runic circle is on.
- You may start Channels outside of a Situation without worrying about an action point cost. 


- **Stable Orbit:** Your Channel starts with 2 mana already stored. The first time your Channel would be disrupted, instead you may choose to lose 2 mana from the Channel. If you cannot do this, the Channel is disrupted. 

- **Crystalline Alignment:** When you Align a channel, you may spend 1AP instead of the spells' normal AP cost to cast any number of spells from the mana stored within, as long as you have mana and AP to cast all of them at once. (e.g. you could cast 4 spells with 4AP and sufficient mana). {Pay no attention to the Unlimited Patience (Ritualist) ability.}

- CHANNELER3: Through the same methods as a Channel, you can create a temporary Teleportation Circle. Choose a destination for the teleportation circle, and then start a Channel. As long as you are in the circle, teleporting a person or object from inside the circle to the chosen destination costs 1 mana. If you are not in the circle, you may have the circle persist as a Sustained spell effect, but the cost of teleporting increases to 2 mana per person/object. The Teleportation Circle is dismissed when you choose to end it, or it runs out of mana.
