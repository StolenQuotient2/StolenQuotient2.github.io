Often it is difficult to come up with an interesting domain for Demigod’s gimmick. The following is a list of Demigod domains for you to borrow from or take inspiration from.   
	I have also included a category called “Niche”. This value represents how difficult it is to adapt the domain into spells for a wide range of situations. Note that, by the nature of the game being fundamentally tied to your own creativity, a domain with a higher Niche value may be harder to play. Domains with a 1 or 5 Niche value should be taken under heavy consideration by both the player and GM before being brought into a game.   
	Domains with an asterisk are domains that were created by Alpha/Beta testers of Misfits, that I have included in this document for posterity. 

| Domain        | Niche | Additional Notes                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| :------------ | :---- | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| (Elemental)   | 2/5   | This is a catch-all for classic elemental domains. Fire, Water, Earth, Lightning, Wind, etc. These are always great options and have a lot of interesting interpretations. Some elements may be shoehorned into certain playstyles or be better adapted for certain situations.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| Combustion\*  | 3/5   | A prime example of “shoehorned into certain playstyles”. This domain very interestingly contains domains like Fire, but doesn’t lend itself well to more passive uses of fire, like for light, or for cooking. There are a number of domains that do well for this; a Domain like Storms\* would encapsulate both Lightning and Wind, but might be bad at charging your phone or cooling you down.                                                                                                                                                        |
| Texture\*     | 4/5   | The prime strategy here was to change surfaces to be advantageous with aid or hinder spells; making the ground slippery or sticky, making blades sharper or duller, etc. This was a remarkably unique and interesting domain when it was played in early testing, and I highly recommend trying it out.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| Gravity\*     | 2/5   | Physics is always a great place to find very versatile domains. This is ranked low on Niche not because it is not specific–you find yourself casting a number of similar spells–but because the immediate application of those spells can be wide ranging. A spell that reduces the gravity on a target can be used both as a positive spell (extra jump height or perhaps flight) or as a negative one (send your enemies or their stuff into the sky). To quote the tester who first played this: “We’re all victims of physics. But you more than me.” |
| Concatenation | 5/5   | This domain is strange, and depends quite a bit on the situation you are in, and the things you’re attempting to concatenate. Concatenating a person’s weapon with the wall works pretty well. Beyond that, things get weird. Concatenating two spells can have interesting results. Concatenating abstract concepts, if your GM allows it, can truly break things. For example, what happens if you concatenate space to itself? {Ask a topologist.}                                                                                                     |
| Forces\*      | 1/5   | Along with any concept deeply tied to physics, a domain like Forces is so wide-ranging that it can quickly stop being interesting. However, restricting the interpretation of what “Force” is to more fantasy-style ideas can work well.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| Sandwich\*    | 4/5   | Yes, you read this right. This domain is a masterclass in picking a lane and sticking to it. The Beta tester who played a demigod with this domain used it to *incredible* effect, using sandwiches of all kinds in order to detect illusions, distract guards, crush people under piles of ham and bread, etc. This domain truly encapsulates the *Misfits* ideology that the only limit to spellcasting is your imagination.                                                                                                                            |
