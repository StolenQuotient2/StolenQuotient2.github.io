The Council is the equivalent of a police force in Panops. Their goal is to solve interpersonal disputes and to protect the people of the cities they occupy. The Council operates some kind of base of operations in nearly every major city in \[Helsings\]. Foundationally, they believe that the use of magic should be restricted to preserve lives and property that would be destroyed by reckless use of magic.   

Surprisingly, the Council is not a part of, nor funded by the Church. However, they have a mutually beneficial relationship. Seized magical artifacts and valuable items that the Council gets from the people they arrest ~~or eliminate~~ are then sold to the Church in order to stock the various dungeons in Sheol. The rest of the Council’s funding comes from those who hire their services for events or otherwise. Council members earn a core salary, but are also paid on commission for artifacts they seize or for extra services they are assigned to do.    

Members volunteer to join the Council and then are trained and employed by them. New Council members must learn and pledge the Code of Order; the Council’s core tenants and operative laws. Council members who break the Code of Order are excommunicated and often banished from the cities they operated in. 

#### **The Code of Order** 

- **“I, \<Name\>, dedicate myself to the preservation of order in the great city of \<City Name\> under the name of the Council.**   
- **I will serve as a shield between the lawful and the unlawful, / protecting the kind, loyal, and trustworthy, / uninhibited by fear or harm.**  
- **I will, with the full extent of my abilities, / enforce that the destructive, the devious and the deceitful, / are brought to swift and efficient justice.**   
- **I will help to create a world of order, peace, and hope, / that, with the God-King’s blessing, / will last for all future generations.”**
