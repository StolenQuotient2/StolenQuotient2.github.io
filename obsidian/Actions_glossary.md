### ***Fate*** 
Starting fate calculation: \+1 if you’re of higher (effective) level, \+1 if allies are assisting, \+1 for any effects influencing your personal abilities in regards to the current action
### **Free Play vs. Situations** 
A *[Situation]({% link obsidian/Situations.md %})* is a subevent of an encounter. Encounters contain one or more Situations. Further clarity requires knowing how actions work. When you’re in an encounter, but not a Situation, you’re in Free Play.

### ***Translating Ability Timings*** 
0-3AP actions can be done in Free Play without concern. Effects that say “once per round” translate to “once per scene”. Abilities that say “once per situation” do not apply in free play. Abilities that say “once between rests/recesses” work as intended

### ***Effective Level***
the party has a level, a players effective level is that number plus any relevant bonuses

### ***Difficulty*** 
Easy actions have difficulty 2\. Standard 3\. Hard 4\. Difficulty 1 and 5 should basically never exist, but 5 might be used for extremely strong enemies
