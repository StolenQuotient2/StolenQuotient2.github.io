### ***Optional: Draincasting Spells***
This is an Optional Rule. Ask your GM if it is being used.   
	As a last-ditch effort, mages can draw upon the life-sustaining mana of their own soul in order to cast spells. Doing so costs 1 Focus, and no mana, but inflicts a Severe injury equal to the potency of the cast spell, plus an additional severe minor injury for every Amplification. You may also Draincast without spending Focus, but it inflicts an additional injury equal to the potency of the spell.
