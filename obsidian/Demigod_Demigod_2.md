* ### ***Gemini ([Demigod]({% link obsidian/Demigod.md %}) \+ Demigod)*** \[\]

  * *The Technique of the Gemini*  
  * *The Technique of the*   
* The following are abilities you may take as a Gemini:  
* {E} GIMMICK: You’ve unlocked a second facet of your soul, previously unknown to you. Pick a second domain. Work with your GM to decide this second domain. {The idea is to avoid “all spells” demigods. Taking the domains of “Order” and “Chaos” for example would allow you to cover nearly every situation. However something like “Fire” and “Ice” is fine, because you can very easily find things outside those two domains, even though they are considered ‘opposites’. Antonyms are cool but the system is not balanced around them.}  
  * Laced Technique: When you cast a spell that falls under 2 or more of your domains, get \+1 Fate.     
  * Integral Derivations: Any domain you can construct through a combination of your two domains can also be considered your domain (e.g. a Demigod of Fire and Ice can reasonably consider Water as a domain of theirs)
