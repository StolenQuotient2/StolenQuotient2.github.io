* ### ***Awakened ([Ley]({% link obsidian/Ley.md %}) \+ Ley) \[\]*** 

* *“They fly now.”*  
* The following are abilities you may take an an Awakened:  
* GIMMICK: By manipulating currents of mana through the air, you can hover and fly short distances. Spend 2 mana each time you start to do this. You move about as fast as you would while running, and cannot scale particularly tall structures in this way, unless you are in an area of heavy leyline activity.   
* Maelstrom: Once per encounter, you can surround yourself and anyone in the near vicinity with a thunderous and swirling storm of mana. The storm is centered on yourself, and moves with you when you walk or run; teleporting or moving at extreme speeds causes the storm to disperse. While inside the Maelstrom, you may activate your (Awakened) GIMMICK without spending mana. Whenever an ally attunes within the storm, instead of any amount of mana that ally would gain, they gain triple the amount in Temporary Mana. The Maelstrom ends whenever you or an ally standing inside the storm ends a round with Temporary Mana.   
* Electro-Magic Pulse: Spend 1 Focus. Until the end of the round, noone in your vicinity can cast spells, including you.
