As part of character creation, you will pick one of five *Origins*. In fiction, these origins describe the source of your magic and how it manifests. Mechanically, the Origin determines what abilities you can take, as well as any starting stats, items and [Talents]({% link obsidian/Talents.md %}) you gain.
