Dream Ward (Guard)  
A small cylinder etched with runes. It emits a nearly imperceptible vibration, and protects an area the size of a small house from divination. Rather than merely hiding those inside its radius, it creates false and misleading answers for anyone trying to locate them by magic.  
Simulacrum of Suffering (Guard)  
The simulacrum starts off as a small, generic looking human figurine. If the same person holds this item for longer than 24 hours, it transforms into a small figurine resembling that person. If that person takes an injury while holding the figurine, they may prevent the injury and the figurine is destroyed instead. When the figuring is destroyed, it lets out a cry in a voice eerily similar to your own.   
Flash Orb (Weapon)  
This perfect sphere appears to be glass, though in truth is far more durable, and swirls with lambent light. Twice between Recesses, you may prime the orb. When primed, it will flash on the wielder's command, releasing bright light and sound, like a flashbang. \<If this item is used during the first action of an encounter, that action has \+1 Fate.\>   
Unlucky Coin (Support)  
Faint magical residue clings to this unusually heavy coin; It has been imbued with gravitational and entropic magic. Any attempt to call its face fails. \<Once between Recesses, when you would Take the Initiative, you may instead Create 3 Opportunities.\> This item nullifies the effect of any \[Lucky Coin\]s held by anyone in the immediate vicinity.   
Band of Spite (Support)  
This iron ring is imbued with a menacing chill. A spellblade wearing this ring finds that attacks they make with their spellblade cannot be inhibited by magical protections that have already been used against them. 

Petrified Squirrel (Support)  
Far from a paperweight, this small statue is the victim of a battle between two powerful mages. It reveals any living, non-human sources of magic in a large radius like a radar. 

Necklace of Hidden Intention (Weapon)  
This necklace has 5 \[1d6+1\] beads filled with a cloudy liquid. At any time, you can pull off a bead and charge it with mana. Spend X mana to charge the bead. The bead then grows into a baseball-sized orb, filled with the cloudy liquid and whatever mana you instill in it (the orb glows blue with ley mana, and yellow with divine mana).   
You may then throw the orb as an action or as part of an action. Wherever the orb lands, cast a spell that costs X mana or less originating from wherever the orb lands. If you choose that the only target of the spell is the point where the orb itself lands, you may cast a spell that costs up to X+4 mana. Afterwards, the orb shatters into glass fragments and cannot be reused.

Opal Crystallized Mana (Support)  
Under the right conditions, Mana can be solidified. It’s not well known what type of Mana is contained within Opal Crystalized Mana, but it generally forms much larger crystals than its blue or yellow counterparts. Opal crystallized mana is translucent and a white-rainbow color. Opal crystalized mana is extremely fragile, and breaks into 5-10 small chunks with anything more than a light tap. Only the full crystal is considered rare, as fragments can be found easily and numerously. A person with Enchantment proficiency can attempt to socket a whole gem into an artifact to increase its enchantability by 10, or a fragment to increase its enchantability by 1\. If all the fragments of a full gem are socketed to the same magical artifact, it gets the full bonus to its enchantability. If the magical artifact loses the gem somehow, it temporarily loses any enchantments it had over its normal enchantability until the crystal is reattached. 	  
Manabound Quill (Oddity)  
A Manabound Quill is alike to a spellblade in many ways–a magical core that draws mana from the air and refines it towards a tip. Unfortunately it is a bit too small to be used as one. A manabound quill can be used to write without a surface, imprinting a temporary trail of mana into the air. It can also be used on a surface to create incredibly long-lasting marks that are difficult to remove.   
A manabound quill can also be used to transfer or create enchantment tattoos on a person. By default, a person can only hold one enchantment at a time.   
Exalted’s Pin (Support)  
A special silver pin that has an impression of a bow and arrow on the front. The pin can only be passively seen by those carrying pins, and can only be worn by someone bound to it. If you are bound to an Exalted’s Pin, once per month you may choose someone to be able to see the pins, even if they are not bound to one themselves. Exalted’s Pins are given to those who are part of the \[Exalted\] as a means of identification.   
Gecko Gloves (Support)  
Gloves with a curious texture on the palm side. Allows the user to free climb any surface, even ceilings. The magic of the gloves extends in such a way that applies to the user's shoes as well.
