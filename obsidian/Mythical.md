**Heretic’s Sword** (Weapon)  
This item can only be bound to by a Demigod with Weapon Proficiency. Supposedly a failed attempt at creating a weapon capable of slaying the God-King, this spellblade grants its wielder the domain of every demigod it has been used to kill. If a demigod wielding this weapon would cast a spell that falls under one or more domains, iterate the cost reduction from the Demigod GIMMICK that many times.

**Child’s Paradox** (Support)  
The Child’s Paradox is an intricately designed and quite large magical device that is designed to rest on someone’s back like a backpack. The large, cylindrical core of the device always seems to be spiraling with a combination of Ley and Divine mana. Pulling the ripcord on the base of the cylinder locks a point in time that you may quickly return to to correct mistakes, as long as they are within a short enough time period (See [The Veil]({% link obsidian/The_Veil.md %}) for why).   
Once between Recesses, pull the ripcord before you take an action. You then proceed to take some number of attempts at an action you describe. The action must be reasonable to complete in about 20s or so. Instead of calculating Fate on the next action, you will instead make any number of Whim rolls in sequence, each of which represents an iteration of your attempts to complete the action.   
After each Whim roll, decide whether or not you want to keep that roll. If you do, that is your score for the action. If you don’t, take a consequence as determined below by the result of that roll, and then reroll. Repeat this process until you choose to stop, or until you cannot take the consequences listed. When you stop, use the latest roll as your roll for the action, unless you have fallen unconscious, in which case score 1\.  

| Result | Consequence |
| :---- | :---- |
| 1 (or less) | Take a Severity 2 injury, lose 2 mana, and lose 1 Focus |
| 2 | Choose two: Severity 2 injury, lose 2 mana, lose 1 Focus |
| 3 (or more) | Choose one: Severity 2 injury, lose 2 mana, lose 1 Focus |

**Codex Kannofel** (Support)  
The Codex Kannofel is a tome created by the Archmage Wainwright Kannofel. In addition to being a typical spellbook, containing notes on high level rituals and other scribblings, the Codex contains a ritual that allows you to enter, exit, and navigate \[The Abyss\] at will.   
In addition, the detailed notes of the late archmage are powerful resources for those studying magic. When you would gain points on the Inspiration track, gain twice that many.

**Rabbit’s Trick** (Oddity)  
The Rabbit’s Trick is a powerful magical storage device. Shaped like a prototypical wizard's hat, the interior of the hat is an extradimensional space that can be used to store objects. The interior of the space is about the size of a closet. However, what makes this object truly special is the fact that it is completely immune to magical detection. You must know the trick is there to be aware that this is an artifact, as opposed to other magical storage devices. Additionally, objects stored inside the hat cannot be divined or otherwise detected by anyone other than the wearer. The artifact otherwise provides no other advantages or stats. The Rabbit’s Trick is currently in possession of the notorious Big Hat. It’s unknown if he took that name before, or after he came into possession of the Rabbit’s Trick.

**Seventh Enigma** (Weapon)  
The Seventh Enigma is a semichaotic magical interference device. The Enigma takes the form of an extremely large amulet, which displays a full crystal of Opal Crystalized Mana that is bound in Leywoven Twine. The amulet emits a large-radius field of entropic magic, which can interfere with the casting of spells in a number of ways. Despite its name, the Seventh Enigma does not share the age or history of the Six Enigmas, which are now under possession of [The Ten]({% link obsidian/Dreamers.md %}).

The Seventh Enigma has five charges, which refresh on taking Recess. Each charge can be used to perform one of the following effects. 

- <!-- OBSIDIAN COMMENT THAT HAS BEEN REMOVED -->
- Grant yourself or an ally \+1 Fate when taking an action that involves dispelling or counteracting a passive spell effect  
- Disable a magic item or active magical effect for one action. This cannot be used on the same effect or item more than Once between Recesses.
