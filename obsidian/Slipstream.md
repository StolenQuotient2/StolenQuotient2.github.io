* ### ***Slipstream ([Ley]({% link obsidian/Ley.md %}) \+ [Spellblade]({% link obsidian/Spellblade.md %})) \[Tenin Episcro\]*** 

  * *“Quote”*  
  * *The Technique of the Slipstream*  
  * *The Technique of the Slipstream is forbidden by the Dreamers and the Council*  
* The following are abilities you may take as a Slipstream:  
* GIMMICK: At any time you may pay 1 mana to teleport a short distance in any direction into an unoccupied space. This teleport must bring you to a place you can see, and thus cannot pass you through solid objects. This ability can be used as part of an action, or as a 0AP action in itself, like walking a short distance.   
  * Breakneck: When you teleport into melee range of an enemy and then make an attack against that enemy, the attack you make is Severe.  
  * Cut in Line: Your GIMMICK allows you to swap places with other people, teleporting to the place they are standing, and having them teleport to the place you just left. You must both be standing on solid ground when you do this.  
  * Phantom: Your GIMMICK works outside of line of sight, and can pass you through thin, mundane objects, like wooden walls or doors. Any sufficiently thick or magically inclined material prevents this.   
  * Whiplash: You can teleport additional people with your GIMMICK that you are holding onto. They must either also pay 1 mana, or suffer a minor injury.
