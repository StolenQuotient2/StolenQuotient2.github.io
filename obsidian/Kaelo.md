For those participating in the Games, or simply running the dungeons for fun, the best place to travel to first is the Kaelo Archipelago, and specifically its capital, Kaelo City {I refuse to be creative here.}. The Archipelago, which is by far the largest in the world, serves as a jumping point for adventurers looking to take on the threats of Sheol, and for avid fans of the Games to spend time in to track the progress of their favorite teams and players.   
	For fans, the island is an information center for the games. The central scoreboard is kept in the center of Kaelo City, which tells the current rankings of all the teams. The archipelago has a number of special taverns which show broadcasts of various high-difficulty dungeons or of high-ranking teams in addition to serving food and drink.   
For adventurers,  the Archipelago is a trade hub. The isles contain hundreds of merchants, willing to buy or sell magic items and tokens. These merchants are primarily run by the Church, who buy extra items off the adventurers to recycle into the dungeons. The Council and Dreamers also run stalls, buying specialized weaponry or enchantment components respectively. There are also a number of private businesses on the island. Private sailors or teleporters are popular, so that adventurers may be able to easily get to any point of Sheol that they wish. Personal enchanters will often go to the island to buy components or sell commissioned pieces of gear.
