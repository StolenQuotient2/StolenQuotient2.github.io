About me

Hi! I'm Sam, I'm a Mathematics and Musical Studies student at Oberlin College. On the math side, I'm primarily an analyst, and on the music side, I'm primarily a composer. This coming fall, I'll be starting a PhD progam at George Mason University.

This website contains information about my professional and academic work, as well as just some general information about myself and my passion projects. If you'd like to learn more about me, you can find a slightly more extensive description about myself here. If you have questions for me—about myself, my work, or anything else—feel free to send me an email.

This website was first created in Summer 2025, so it remains a bit bare. As I continue to do interesting things I will continue to update this site. Feel free to poke around regardless!
